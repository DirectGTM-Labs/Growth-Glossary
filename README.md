# Growth Glossary

A structured, open glossary of Growth, GTM, Growth Engineering,
Growth Hacking, MarTech, SEO, GEO, SEM, SMO, and related disciplines.

Part of [DirectGTM-Labs].

## About

Growth Glossary is a structured knowledge project documenting
the terminology, concepts, metrics, frameworks, technologies,
and practices used across the modern Growth ecosystem.

The project aims to provide clear and useful definitions for
professionals, practitioners, learners, and researchers.

## Scope

The glossary covers areas including:

- Growth
- Go-to-Market (GTM)
- Growth Engineering
- Growth Hacking
- MarTech
- SEO
- GEO
- SEM
- SMO
- Performance Marketing
- Product Growth
- Lifecycle Marketing
- Analytics
- Experimentation
- Conversion Rate Optimization
- Customer Acquisition
- Retention
- Monetization
- and related disciplines.

## Structure

Each glossary entry is maintained as an individual Markdown document.

Entries may be associated with multiple domains where concepts
overlap across disciplines.
