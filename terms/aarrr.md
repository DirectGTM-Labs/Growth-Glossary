# AARRR

## Definition

AARRR is a growth measurement framework organized around Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, and Revenue. It is a diagnostic model rather than a universal causal law.

## 中文定义

传统线性漏斗：Acquisition(获客), Activation(激活), Retention(留存), Referral(推荐), Revenue(变现)。

## Why It Matters

Understanding AARRR helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use AARRR to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
