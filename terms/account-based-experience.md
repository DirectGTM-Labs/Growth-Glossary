# Account-Based Experience (ABX)

## Definition

Account-Based Experience (ABX) coordinates marketing, product, sales, and service experiences around specific target accounts rather than treating every visitor as an undifferentiated audience.

## 中文定义

客户账户体验是与“Account-Based Experience (ABX)”对应的专业概念，用于描述该术语所代表的业务、工程、数据、实验或增长机制。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Account-Based Experience (ABX) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Account-Based Experience (ABX) to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
