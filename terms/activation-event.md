# Activation Event

## Definition

A specific user action or milestone chosen to represent the point at which a new user has experienced meaningful initial value.

## 中文定义

激活事件是团队明确选定的一个关键用户行为，用来代表新用户已经首次体验到产品的核心价值。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Activation Event by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
