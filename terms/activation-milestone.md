# Activation Milestone

## Definition

A defined set or sequence of early behaviors that indicates a new user has progressed toward sustained product value.

## 中文定义

激活里程碑是由一个或多个早期关键行为构成的阶段性标准，用于判断新用户是否正在接近稳定获得产品价值的状态。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Activation Milestone by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
