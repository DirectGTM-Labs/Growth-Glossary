# Ad Rank

## Definition

Google Ads' auction-time value used to determine whether an ad is eligible to show and where it can appear, based on multiple signals including bid, ad and landing-page quality, thresholds, context, and expected asset impact.

## 中文定义

广告排名是 Google Ads 在每次竞价中用于判断广告是否有资格展示以及展示位置的综合值，会结合出价、广告和落地页质量、竞价环境、阈值和素材预期影响等因素。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Ad Rank alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
