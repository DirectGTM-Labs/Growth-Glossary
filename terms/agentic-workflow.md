# Agentic Workflow

## Definition

An agentic workflow coordinates one or more AI agents, models, tools, and control steps to execute a multi-step task with conditional or iterative behavior.

## 中文定义

让多个 AI Agent 像公司员工一样互相交接工作（例如：Agent1爬取对手价格，Agent2分析策略，Agent3自动调价）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Agentic Workflow helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Agentic Workflow into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
