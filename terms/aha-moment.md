# Aha Moment

## Definition

An Aha Moment is the point at which a user first recognizes or experiences the core value of a product or feature.

## 中文定义

用户迷糊了两分钟后，在用某个功能的瞬间感受到多巴胺直冲脑门：“卧槽，这东西原来这么好用！”

## Why It Matters

Understanding Aha Moment helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Aha Moment to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
