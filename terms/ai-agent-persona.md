# AI Agent Persona

## Definition

An AI agent persona is a defined role, behavioral profile, communication style, and operating context assigned to an AI agent for a particular workflow.

## 中文定义

AI代理人设。给大语言模型挂上：“你是个刻薄且极度专业的医疗保险索赔精算老油条”来锁住语气的灵魂。

## Why It Matters

Understanding AI Agent Persona helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use AI Agent Persona to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
