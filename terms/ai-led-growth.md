# AI-Led Growth

## Definition

AI-led growth is a proposed growth model in which AI systems or agents materially participate in acquisition, product delivery, customer interaction, or commercial execution.

## 中文定义

2026 前沿范式。靠你公司的 AI Agent 直接进驻各种大模型应用商城，自动帮用户完成端到端的任务甚至采购闭环。

## Why It Matters

Understanding AI-Led Growth helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use AI-Led Growth to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
