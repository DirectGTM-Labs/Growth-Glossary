# AI Visibility

## Definition

A measurement concept describing how often and how prominently a brand, entity, product, or source appears across a defined set of AI-generated answers.

## 中文定义

AI 可见度用于衡量品牌、实体、产品或来源在一组预先定义的 AI 问答场景中出现的频率和显著程度。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate AI Visibility across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
