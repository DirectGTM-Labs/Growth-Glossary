# Significance Level (Alpha)

## Definition

The pre-specified probability threshold for Type I error in a classical hypothesis test under its assumptions.

## 中文定义

Alpha 显著性水平是在经典假设检验中事先规定的第一类错误概率控制阈值。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Significance Level (Alpha) in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
