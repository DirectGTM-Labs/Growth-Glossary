# Anchoring Effect

## Definition

The anchoring effect is the tendency for an initial value or reference point to influence subsequent judgments and estimates.

## 中文定义

锚定效应。第一眼看到划掉的天价 12999，你就会极度失去理智地买下旁边的 599 垃圾。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Anchoring Effect helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Anchoring Effect to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
