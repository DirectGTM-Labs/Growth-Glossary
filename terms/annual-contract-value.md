# Annual Contract Value (ACV)

## Definition

The annualized recurring value of a customer contract, often used to compare deal sizes across contracts with different durations.

## 中文定义

年度合同价值是把一份客户合同的经常性价值换算为年度口径后的金额，便于比较不同合同期限的交易规模。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Annual Contract Value (ACV) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
