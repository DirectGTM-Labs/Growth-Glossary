# Annual Plan

## Definition

A subscription term billed or committed for approximately one year, often with different economics from month-to-month service.

## 中文定义

年度方案是以约一年为承诺或计费周期的订阅方案，其折扣、现金流和流失特征通常与月度方案不同。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Annual Plan when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
