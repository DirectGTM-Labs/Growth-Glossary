# Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

## Definition

An industry term for improving content so answer-oriented search systems can identify, extract, and present useful responses; its scope overlaps with SEO and GEO and is not governed by a single universal standard.

## 中文定义

答案引擎优化是业界用于描述“让内容更容易被答案型搜索系统识别、抽取和呈现”的术语，其边界与 SEO、GEO 有重叠，目前并不存在统一行业标准。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
