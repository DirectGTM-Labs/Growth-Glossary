# ARPU

## Definition

Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) is revenue over a defined period divided by the relevant user population, with the population definition stated explicitly.

## 中文定义

简单粗暴，用大盘总收入除以活着的总人头数。

## Why It Matters

Understanding ARPU helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use ARPU to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
