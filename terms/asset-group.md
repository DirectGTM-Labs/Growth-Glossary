# Asset Group

## Definition

A collection of creative assets, audience signals, and related settings used within campaign types such as Performance Max.

## 中文定义

素材资源组是在 Performance Max 等广告系列中，把图片、文本、视频、受众信号及相关配置组织在一起的素材集合。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Asset Group alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
