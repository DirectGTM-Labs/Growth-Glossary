# Assignment Unit

## Definition

The entity to which an experimental condition is assigned; in many experiments it is the same as the randomization unit, but systems should define the distinction explicitly when they differ.

## 中文定义

分配单位是实验处理实际被分配到的实体；它经常与随机化单位一致，但在复杂实验中两者需要明确区分。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Assignment Unit in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
