# Attribution Window

## Definition

An attribution window is the defined time period during which an interaction can receive credit for a later conversion under an attribution model.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Attribution Window helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Attribution Window to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
