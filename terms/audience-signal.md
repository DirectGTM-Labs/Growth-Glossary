# Audience Signal

## Definition

A set of audience inputs that can help automated campaigns learn which users may be relevant, without operating as a strict targeting boundary in every context.

## 中文定义

受众信号是向自动化广告系列提供的潜在受众线索，用于帮助系统学习可能相关的用户，但在很多场景下并不等同于严格的受众边界。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Audience Signal alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
