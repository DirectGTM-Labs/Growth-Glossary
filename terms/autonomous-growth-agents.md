# Autonomous Growth Agents

## Definition

Autonomous Growth Agents are AI-enabled software agents designed to perform parts of a growth workflow—such as analysis, hypothesis generation, content or code drafting, or experiment operations—with varying degrees of human oversight.

## 中文定义

自主增长智能体是利用 AI 执行部分增长工作流的软件智能体，例如分析、生成假设、起草内容或代码、辅助实验运营；其自主程度取决于具体系统和人类监督机制。

## Why It Matters

Agents can reduce repetitive analytical and operational work, but growth decisions require explicit permissions, measurement quality, privacy controls, and human or automated guardrails.

## Example

An agent queries an approved analytics dataset, identifies an unusual funnel drop, drafts a hypothesis and SQL analysis, and submits the proposed experiment for human approval.
