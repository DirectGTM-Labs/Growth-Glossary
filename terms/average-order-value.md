# Average Order Value (AOV)

## Definition

Average revenue generated per completed order during a defined period.

## 中文定义

平均订单价值是在规定周期内，总订单收入除以完成订单数得到的平均每单收入。

## Formula

AOV = Revenue / Number of Orders

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Average Order Value (AOV) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
