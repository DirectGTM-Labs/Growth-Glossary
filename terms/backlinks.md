# Backlinks

## Definition

Backlinks are hyperlinks from external pages to a target page and can contribute to discovery, authority signals, and referral traffic.

## 中文定义

外链/反向链接。整个互联网里其他各种野外高权重网站上放着的指向你家的超链接，是最大的搜索引擎投票。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Backlinks helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
