# Behavioral Segmentation

## Definition

Behavioral segmentation groups users or customers by observed actions such as purchase frequency, feature use, engagement, or lifecycle behavior.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Behavioral Segmentation helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Behavioral Segmentation to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
