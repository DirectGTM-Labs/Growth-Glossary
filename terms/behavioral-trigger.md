# Behavioral Trigger

## Definition

A user or account behavior used to initiate an automated experience, message, audience update, or sales action.

## 中文定义

行为触发器是由用户或账户发生的某项行为所形成的自动化条件，可用于启动消息、受众更新、产品体验或销售动作。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Behavioral Trigger in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
