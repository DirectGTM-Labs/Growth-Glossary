# Blue Ocean Strategy

## Definition

Blue Ocean Strategy is a strategic framework associated with creating or reconstructing market space where competition is less directly comparable, rather than competing solely within established boundaries.

## 中文定义

别去血海拼价格，通过重组价值链，创造一个毫无对手的全新游戏规则空地（如最初的特斯拉）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Blue Ocean Strategy helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Blue Ocean Strategy to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
