# Bonferroni Correction

## Definition

A conservative multiple-testing method that controls family-wise error by dividing the desired alpha across the number of tested hypotheses.

## 中文定义

邦费罗尼校正是一种较保守的多重检验方法，通过把整体 Alpha 分配到多个假设上控制家族错误率。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Bonferroni Correction in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
