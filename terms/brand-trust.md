# Brand Trust

## Definition

Brand trust is the degree to which customers believe a brand is reliable, honest, competent, and likely to act consistently with expectations.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Brand Trust helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
