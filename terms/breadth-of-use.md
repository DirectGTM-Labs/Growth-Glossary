# Breadth of Use

## Definition

The number or diversity of distinct product features, modules, or use cases adopted by a user or account.

## 中文定义

使用广度描述单个用户或账户实际采用了多少种不同功能、模块或使用场景。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Breadth of Use by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
