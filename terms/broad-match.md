# Broad Match

## Definition

A Google Ads keyword match type that can match searches related to a keyword's meaning, using additional contextual signals beyond literal wording.

## 中文定义

广泛匹配是 Google Ads 的关键词匹配方式，可基于关键词含义及其他上下文信号匹配更广泛的相关搜索，而不要求字面完全出现。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Broad Match alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
