# Buying Committee

## Definition

A buying committee is the group of stakeholders who influence, approve, procure, use, or block a purchase within an organization.

## 中文定义

采购委员会。B2B 的终极恶梦——买个软件不仅得销售点赞，还得过法务、熬安全审计、搞定最抠的财务。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Buying Committee helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Buying Committee to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
