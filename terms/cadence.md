# Growth Team Cadence

## Definition

Cadence is the recurring rhythm of planning, reviewing, decision-making, experimentation, and learning activities that keeps a growth team operating consistently.

## 中文定义

团队运行节奏（Cadence）是增长团队通过周期性的规划、评审、决策、实验和学习活动保持稳定运行的工作节奏。

## Why It Matters

A consistent cadence turns experimentation into an operating system rather than a series of isolated projects.

## Example

A team reviews experiment results weekly, prioritizes the next hypotheses, and records decisions and learnings in a shared system.
