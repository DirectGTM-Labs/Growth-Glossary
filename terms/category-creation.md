# Category Creation

## Definition

Category creation is the strategic process of defining and establishing a new market category, including its problem framing, terminology, buyer expectations, and competitive boundaries.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Category Creation helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Category Creation to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
