# Choice Architecture

## Definition

Choice architecture is the design of how options are presented and ordered, which can influence decisions without changing the underlying options.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Choice Architecture helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Choice Architecture to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
