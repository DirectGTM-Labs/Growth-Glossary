# Document Chunking

## Definition

The process of splitting documents into retrieval-sized units while preserving enough semantic context for search or RAG.

## 中文定义

文档切块是把长文档拆分成适合检索和上下文输入的小单元，同时尽量保持每个单元的语义完整性。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate Document Chunking across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
