# Churn Rate

## Definition

Churn rate measures the proportion of customers, users, or revenue lost during a defined period relative to an appropriate starting or at-risk population.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Churn Rate helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Churn Rate to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
