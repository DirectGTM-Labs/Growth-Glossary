# Churned MRR

## Definition

Monthly recurring revenue lost when customers fully cancel or terminate recurring subscriptions.

## 中文定义

流失 MRR 是客户彻底取消或终止订阅后损失的月度经常性收入。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Churned MRR in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
