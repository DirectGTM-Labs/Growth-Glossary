# CI/CD

## Definition

CI/CD is the practice of automating software integration, testing, delivery, and/or deployment so changes can move through a controlled pipeline.

## 中文定义

工程师写完代码一点提交，系统自动跑全套测试兵并安全发布到外网的自动化流水线。

## Why It Matters

Understanding CI/CD helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate CI/CD into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
