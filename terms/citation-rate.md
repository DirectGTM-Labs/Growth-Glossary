# Citation Rate

## Definition

Citation rate is the proportion or frequency of eligible answers, documents, or retrieval contexts in which a source, entity, or content asset is cited; the exact denominator must be defined.

## 中文定义

引用率（GEO核心 KPI）。你的内容被世界顶尖大模型在回答里挂在 `[1]` 上尊贵引用的频次。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Citation Rate helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Citation Rate to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
