# Click Map

## Definition

A visualization showing where users click or tap on a page or interface.

## 中文定义

点击图是显示用户在页面或界面哪些位置发生点击或触摸，以及相对密度的可视化。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Click Map to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
