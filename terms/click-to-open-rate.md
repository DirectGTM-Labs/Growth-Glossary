# Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)

## Definition

The percentage of recorded opens that also generate at least one click, used as a rough measure of content engagement among openers.

## 中文定义

CTOR 是已经被记录为打开的邮件中，进一步产生至少一次点击的比例，常用于粗略观察打开后的内容互动。

## Formula

CTOR = Unique Clickers / Recorded Unique Opens

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR) in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
