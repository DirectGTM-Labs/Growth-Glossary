# CLV/CAC Ratio

## Definition

The CLV/CAC ratio compares estimated customer lifetime value with customer acquisition cost and is used as one lens on acquisition economics; the appropriate definition of CLV and CAC matters.

## 中文定义

行业黄金法则是 3:1（赚3块花1块）。如果到了 1:1 公司即刻倒闭，如果高达 10:1 说明你极度保守错失了占领地球的机会。

## Why It Matters

Understanding CLV/CAC Ratio helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use CLV/CAC Ratio to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
