# Cognitive Bias

## Definition

A cognitive bias is a systematic tendency in judgment or decision-making that can produce deviations from normative reasoning under particular conditions.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Cognitive Bias helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

For example, a growth team could use Cognitive Bias to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
