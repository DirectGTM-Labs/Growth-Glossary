# Cognitive Load

## Definition

Cognitive load is the mental processing demand imposed by a task or interface; excessive unnecessary load can impair comprehension and decision-making.

## 中文定义

让用户看一眼网页需要动用的脑力。选项越多、文字越密集，负荷越高，用户会瞬间崩溃跳出。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Cognitive Load helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Cognitive Load to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
