# Community-led Growth

## Definition

Community-led growth uses user communities, peer interaction, shared knowledge, and member participation as important mechanisms for acquisition, retention, trust, or expansion.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Community-led Growth helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Community-led Growth to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
