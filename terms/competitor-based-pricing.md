# Competitor-Based Pricing

## Definition

A pricing approach that uses competitors' prices and market positioning as a primary reference point.

## 中文定义

竞争导向定价是把竞争对手价格和市场定位作为主要参照来设计自身价格的方法。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Competitor-Based Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
