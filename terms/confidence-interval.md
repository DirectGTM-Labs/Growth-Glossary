# Confidence Interval

## Definition

A confidence interval is an interval-estimation procedure that, under repeated sampling and its assumptions, has a specified long-run coverage probability for the parameter.

## 中文定义

给出一组范围：“我们 95% 确信这次改动能带来 2.1% 到 4.3% 的提升”。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Confidence Interval helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Confidence Interval into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
