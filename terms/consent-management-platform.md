# Consent Management Platform (CMP)

## Definition

A system used to collect, store, enforce, and communicate user consent or privacy preferences across websites, apps, and downstream technologies.

## 中文定义

同意管理平台用于采集、保存、执行并向下游系统传递用户的隐私同意和偏好状态。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Consent Management Platform (CMP) so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
