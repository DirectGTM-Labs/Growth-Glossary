# Consent Mode

## Definition

A mechanism for adjusting the behavior of measurement or advertising tags based on a user's consent state while supporting privacy-aware measurement.

## 中文定义

同意模式是根据用户的同意状态调整分析或广告标签行为，并在隐私约束下支持测量的一类机制。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Consent Mode so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
