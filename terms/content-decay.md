# Content Decay

## Definition

A decline in a page's organic visibility, traffic, relevance, or conversion performance over time.

## 中文定义

内容衰减是既有页面随着时间推移出现自然搜索曝光、流量、相关性或转化能力持续下降的现象。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Content Decay during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
