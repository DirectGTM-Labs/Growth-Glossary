# Content-led Growth

## Definition

Content-led growth uses useful content as a primary mechanism for discovery, education, acquisition, activation, or retention.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Content-led Growth helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Content-led Growth to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
