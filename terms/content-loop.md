# Content Loop

## Definition

A content loop is a growth loop in which user- or company-generated content creates discovery or demand that brings in additional users who then create more content.

## 中文定义

用户进来发贴->帖子被 Google 免费收录拿流量->新人搜索点进来发贴，形成知乎式的永动机闭环。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Content Loop helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Content Loop to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
