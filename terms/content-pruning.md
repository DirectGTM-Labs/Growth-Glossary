# Content Pruning

## Definition

The deliberate removal, consolidation, redirecting, or deindexing of low-value or redundant content after evidence-based review.

## 中文定义

内容修剪是在数据和内容质量判断基础上，对低价值、重复或无战略意义页面进行删除、合并、重定向或 noindex 处理。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Content Pruning during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
