# Content Repurposing

## Definition

The adaptation of an existing content asset into new formats, cuts, channels, or audience-specific versions.

## 中文定义

内容再利用是把已有内容改编成新的格式、长度、渠道版本或针对不同受众的衍生内容，以提高内容资产利用率。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply Content Repurposing by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
