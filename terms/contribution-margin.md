# Contribution Margin

## Definition

Revenue remaining after subtracting variable costs directly attributable to delivering the product or serving the customer.

## 中文定义

贡献毛利是收入扣除与产品交付或服务客户直接相关的可变成本后剩余的金额或比例。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Contribution Margin in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
