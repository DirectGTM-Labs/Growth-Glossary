# Conversational Marketing

## Definition

Conversational marketing uses interactive dialogue, often through messaging, chat, or AI assistants, to help qualify, educate, support, or convert prospects and customers.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Conversational Marketing helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Conversational Marketing to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
