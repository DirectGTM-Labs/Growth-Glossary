# Conversion Action

## Definition

A configured business action in an advertising or analytics platform that is measured as a conversion, such as a purchase, qualified lead, or signup.

## 中文定义

转化操作是在广告或分析平台中被配置为有业务价值的动作，例如购买、合格线索、注册或电话。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Conversion Action alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
