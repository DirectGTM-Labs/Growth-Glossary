# Conversion Value

## Definition

A numeric value assigned to a conversion so campaigns can optimize and report based on economic value rather than conversion count alone.

## 中文定义

转化价值是给某次转化赋予的数值，使广告系统能够根据经济价值而不仅仅是转化次数进行衡量和优化。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Conversion Value alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
