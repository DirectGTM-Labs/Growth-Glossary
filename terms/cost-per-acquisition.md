# Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

## Definition

Cost per acquisition/action (CPA) is the cost associated with generating a defined acquisition or conversion action, typically calculated as relevant spend divided by completed actions.

## 中文定义

每次行动成本是与“Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)”对应的专业概念，用于描述该术语所代表的业务、工程、数据、实验或增长机制。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
