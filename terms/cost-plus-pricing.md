# Cost-Plus Pricing

## Definition

A pricing approach that adds a target markup to the cost of producing or delivering a product.

## 中文定义

成本加成定价是在产品生产或交付成本基础上加上目标利润率或固定加价来确定价格的方法。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Cost-Plus Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
