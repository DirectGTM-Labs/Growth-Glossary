# Counterfactual Logging

## Definition

Counterfactual logging records information needed to evaluate what would have happened under alternative decisions or treatments, enabling later causal or policy analysis.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Counterfactual Logging helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
