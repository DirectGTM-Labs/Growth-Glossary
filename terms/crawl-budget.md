# Crawl Budget

## Definition

Crawl budget is the practical amount of crawling a search engine is willing or able to allocate to a site over time, influenced by factors such as capacity and crawl demand.

## 中文定义

搜索引擎分配给网站的爬虫访问量上限。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Crawl Budget helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
