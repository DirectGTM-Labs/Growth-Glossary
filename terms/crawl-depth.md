# Crawl Depth

## Definition

The number of link steps required to reach a page from a chosen starting page such as the homepage or a major hub.

## 中文定义

抓取深度表示从主页或某个核心入口页面出发，需要经过多少次链接跳转才能到达目标页面。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Crawl Depth during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
