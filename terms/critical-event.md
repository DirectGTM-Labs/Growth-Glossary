# Critical Event

## Definition

An event in the user journey that has disproportionate diagnostic or predictive importance for activation, retention, conversion, or monetization.

## 中文定义

关键事件是对激活、转化、留存或变现具有较强诊断意义或预测价值的用户事件。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Critical Event by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
