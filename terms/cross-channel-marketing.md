# Cross-Channel Marketing

## Definition

A marketing approach that coordinates messages across multiple channels while preserving context between touchpoints.

## 中文定义

跨渠道营销是在多个营销渠道中协调沟通内容和上下文，使用户在不同触点之间能够获得相互关联的体验。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Cross-Channel Marketing in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
