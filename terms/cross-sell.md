# Cross-sell

## Definition

Cross-sell is the sale of an additional complementary product or service to an existing customer.

## 中文定义

交叉销售。买旗舰版的同时，店员立刻顺手推销搭配使用的原厂耳机包。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Cross-sell helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Cross-sell to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
