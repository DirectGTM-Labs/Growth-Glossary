# CSAT

## Definition

CSAT is a concept, method, technology, metric, or practice used in Growth Hacking. In this glossary, it refers to the defined mechanism or operating idea represented by the term, with its scope and assumptions stated explicitly rather than treating the term as a universal rule. The source material associates it with: 本次服务结束后立刻弹出的五星好评打分.

## Why It Matters

Understanding CSAT helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
