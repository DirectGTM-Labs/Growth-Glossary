# CUPED

## Definition

A variance-reduction technique that uses pre-experiment covariates correlated with the outcome to improve precision in randomized experiments.

## 中文定义

CUPED 是一种实验方差缩减方法，利用与结果相关的实验前协变量降低估计噪声，从而提高随机实验的统计精度。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document CUPED in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
