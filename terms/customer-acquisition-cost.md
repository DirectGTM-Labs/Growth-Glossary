# Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

## Definition

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is the average acquisition cost attributable to each newly acquired customer over a defined period and according to a defined cost scope.

## 中文定义

客户获取成本（CAC）是在明确时间范围和成本口径下，平均分摊到每个新获取客户身上的获取成本。

## Formula

CAC = Total Acquisition Costs / Number of New Customers Acquired

## Why It Matters

CAC is a core unit-economics metric for evaluating acquisition efficiency and the economics of scaling a customer base.

## Example

If $10,000 of defined acquisition costs produce 100 new customers, CAC is $100 per customer.
