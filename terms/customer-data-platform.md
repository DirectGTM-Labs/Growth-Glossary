# Customer Data Platform (CDP)

## Definition

A system designed to collect, unify, govern, and activate customer data across sources for analytics and customer engagement use cases.

## 中文定义

客户数据平台是用于跨来源采集、统一、治理并激活客户数据，以支持分析、分群和客户互动的系统。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Customer Data Platform (CDP) in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
