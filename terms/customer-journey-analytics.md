# Customer Journey Analytics

## Definition

The analysis of behavior and outcomes across multiple touchpoints and channels to understand paths, friction, progression, and conversion.

## 中文定义

客户旅程分析通过关联多个触点和渠道中的行为与结果，研究用户路径、摩擦、阶段推进和转化。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Customer Journey Analytics so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
