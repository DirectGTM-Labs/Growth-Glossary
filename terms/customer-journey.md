# Customer Journey

## Definition

The sequence of interactions and experiences a person or account has with a company across discovery, evaluation, purchase, use, renewal, and advocacy.

## 中文定义

客户旅程是个人或账户从发现、评估、购买、使用到续约和推荐过程中与企业发生的一系列跨触点互动。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Customer Journey in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
