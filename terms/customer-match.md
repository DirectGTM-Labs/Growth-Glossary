# Customer Match

## Definition

A Google Ads audience capability that uses consented first-party customer data to match users for supported targeting, observation, exclusion, and bidding use cases.

## 中文定义

客户匹配是利用经过授权的第一方客户数据，在 Google Ads 中进行匹配，从而支持符合政策要求的受众定位、观察、排除或出价场景。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Customer Match alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
