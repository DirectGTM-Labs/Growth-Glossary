# Customer Success

## Definition

Customer success is a function focused on helping customers achieve desired outcomes and thereby supporting retention, adoption, expansion, and long-term value.

## 中文定义

B2B 中绝不是客服。这是个背负续费指标的铁军，天天跟在掏了大钱的客户屁股后面逼他把软件用好，防着他跑路。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Customer Success helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Customer Success to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
