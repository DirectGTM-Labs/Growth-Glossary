# Dark Funnel

## Definition

Dark funnel refers to buyer research and influence occurring in channels that are difficult for conventional attribution systems to observe, such as private communities, peer conversations, or offline interactions.

## 中文定义

## Why It Matters

Understanding Dark Funnel helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Dark Funnel to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
