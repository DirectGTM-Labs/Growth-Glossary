# Dark Patterns

## Definition

Dark patterns are user-interface or interaction designs that materially impair users' ability to make informed, freely chosen decisions by using deception, manipulation, coercion, or obstruction.

## 中文定义

暗黑模式是通过欺骗、操纵、强迫或设置障碍等方式，实质性削弱用户知情并自由做出选择能力的界面或交互设计。

## Why It Matters

Short-term conversion gains from manipulative design can create legal, ethical, trust, retention, and brand risks.

## Example

A subscription flow makes the paid option prominent while obscuring or confusing the path to decline or cancel.
