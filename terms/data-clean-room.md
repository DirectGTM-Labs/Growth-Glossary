# Data Clean Room

## Definition

A controlled environment that allows multiple parties to perform approved analysis on joined datasets while restricting direct access to underlying sensitive data.

## 中文定义

数据洁净室是在受控环境中允许多方对联合数据执行经过批准的分析，同时限制参与方直接访问原始敏感数据的技术和治理模式。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Data Clean Room so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
