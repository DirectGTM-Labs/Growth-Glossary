# Data-Driven Attribution (DDA)

## Definition

Data-driven attribution uses algorithmic or statistical methods to estimate the relative contribution of multiple marketing touchpoints to a conversion or outcome.

## 中文定义

数据驱动归因是与“Data-Driven Attribution (DDA)”对应的专业概念，用于描述该术语所代表的业务、工程、数据、实验或增长机制。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Data-Driven Attribution (DDA) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Data-Driven Attribution (DDA) to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
