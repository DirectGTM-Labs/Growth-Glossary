# Data Lineage

## Definition

The traceable history of how data moves and transforms from source systems through pipelines, models, and downstream reports or applications.

## 中文定义

数据血缘记录数据从源系统经过管道、转换、模型一直到报表和应用的完整流转与加工路径。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Data Lineage so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
