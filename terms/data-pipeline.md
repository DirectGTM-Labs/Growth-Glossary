# Data Pipeline

## Definition

A data pipeline is a system or workflow that moves data from source systems through ingestion, transport, processing, and storage or delivery to downstream consumers.

## 中文定义

数据管道是将数据从源系统经过摄取、传输、处理，再存储或交付给下游使用者的一套系统或工作流程。

## Why It Matters

Reliable pipelines turn raw product and business events into data that analytics, experimentation, and operational systems can use.

## Example

Product events are collected, validated, transformed, loaded into a warehouse, and then exposed to BI models.
