# Data Quality

## Definition

The degree to which data is accurate, complete, consistent, timely, valid, and fit for its intended analytical or operational purpose.

## 中文定义

数据质量表示数据在准确性、完整性、一致性、及时性、有效性以及是否适合实际用途等方面的水平。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Data Quality so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
