# Data Warehouse

## Definition

A data warehouse is a centralized analytical data platform optimized for storing and querying structured or modeled data for reporting, analytics, and business intelligence.

## 中文定义

数据仓库是面向分析的数据平台，用于集中存储和查询结构化或经过建模的数据，服务于报表、分析和商业智能。

## Why It Matters

A warehouse provides a governed analytical environment where teams can define reusable models and metrics.

## Example

A company loads product events and billing data into BigQuery and builds modeled tables for cohort and revenue analysis.
