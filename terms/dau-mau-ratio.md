# DAU/MAU Ratio

## Definition

DAU/MAU is the ratio of daily active users to monthly active users and is often used as a coarse measure of frequency or engagement; interpretation depends strongly on product usage cadence.

## 中文定义

每天打开的人数除以每个月打开的人数。如果大于 20%，说明产品极好；如果接近 1%，说明是个被遗忘的工具箱。

## Why It Matters

Understanding DAU/MAU Ratio helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use DAU/MAU Ratio to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
