# Email Delivery Rate

## Definition

The percentage of accepted send attempts that are not rejected as bounces, which does not guarantee inbox placement.

## 中文定义

邮件投递率是发送尝试中未被邮件服务器拒绝的比例，但成功投递并不意味着邮件一定进入主收件箱。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Email Delivery Rate in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
