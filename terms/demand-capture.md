# Demand Capture

## Definition

Activities that convert existing market intent into identifiable pipeline or revenue by meeting buyers when they are actively searching or evaluating.

## 中文定义

需求捕获是在用户已经产生搜索、比较或采购意图时，通过搜索、内容、渠道或销售承接，把现有需求转化为线索、商机或收入。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Demand Capture in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
