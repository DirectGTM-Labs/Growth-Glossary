# Demand Generation

## Definition

A full-funnel discipline for creating, developing, and capturing market demand through coordinated brand, content, paid, product, and sales programs.

## 中文定义

需求生成是围绕完整买家旅程，通过品牌、内容、付费、产品和销售协同来创造、培育并捕获市场需求的系统性增长职能。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Demand Generation in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
