# Demand Waterfall

## Definition

Demand waterfall is a process model for progressing marketing-generated demand through qualification, scoring, routing, and sales acceptance stages.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Demand Waterfall helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Demand Waterfall to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
