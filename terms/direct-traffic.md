# Direct Traffic

## Definition

Direct traffic is a web-analytics classification for visits for which no reliable referring source is recorded; it does not necessarily mean the user manually typed the URL.

## 中文定义

直接流量。不靠搜索不靠朋友，他直接在浏览器强行默写出 `你的网址.com` 回来的人，忠诚度拉满。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Direct Traffic helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Direct Traffic to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
