# Display Ads

## Definition

Display ads are visual advertisements delivered through websites, apps, or advertising networks, typically using banners, native units, rich media, or similar formats.

## 中文定义

展示广告。各种低端网站边角料和 App 开屏时闪出的大图硬广海报。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Display Ads helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Display Ads to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
