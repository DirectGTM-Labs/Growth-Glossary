# DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)

## Definition

An email-authentication mechanism that adds a cryptographic signature so receiving systems can verify message integrity and domain authorization.

## 中文定义

DKIM 是通过数字签名验证邮件内容完整性以及发送域名授权关系的邮件认证机制。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
