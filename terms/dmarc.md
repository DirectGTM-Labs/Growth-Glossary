# DMARC

## Definition

A domain-level email policy and reporting standard that builds on SPF and DKIM to define alignment and handling of authentication failures.

## 中文定义

DMARC 是建立在 SPF 与 DKIM 之上的域名级邮件认证和报告标准，用来定义对齐要求以及认证失败邮件的处理策略。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize DMARC in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
