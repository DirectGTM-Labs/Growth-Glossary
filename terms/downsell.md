# Downsell

## Definition

Downsell is a retention-oriented transition to a lower-priced or lower-capacity offer when a customer is unwilling or unable to maintain the current plan.

## 中文定义

客户点进退款页面准备跑路，系统立刻红字弹出：“实在太贵对吧？那给你降级到乞丐版，每月只要 1 块钱，求你别走！”

## Why It Matters

Understanding Downsell helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Downsell to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
