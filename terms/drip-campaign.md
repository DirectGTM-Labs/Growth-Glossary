# Drip Campaign

## Definition

A drip campaign sends a predefined or rule-based sequence of communications over time, often triggered by signup, behavior, or lifecycle stage.

## 中文定义

像医院打点滴一样。第一天发感谢信，第三天发干货，第七天发折扣。事先排好全自动慢慢浸透用户心智的邮件流。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Drip Campaign helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Drip Campaign to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
