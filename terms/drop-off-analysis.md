# Drop-off Analysis

## Definition

Drop-off analysis identifies where users discontinue a defined journey, funnel, workflow, or product experience.

## 中文定义

查清究竟是在填收货地址，还是在绑定信用卡时，一大批用户绝望地关闭了网页。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Drop-off Analysis helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Drop-off Analysis into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
