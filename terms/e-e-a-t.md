# E-E-A-T

## Definition

E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness, a set of quality concepts Google discusses in its search quality guidance; it is not a single ranking score.

## 中文定义

经验、专业、权威、可信。Google 决定是否把你的内容打入冷宫的四大最高级玄学审核标准（针对医疗/金融尤为严苛）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding E-E-A-T helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use E-E-A-T to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
