# Edge Experimentation

## Definition

Edge experimentation performs experiment assignment or related logic at or near the network edge, such as CDN or edge-runtime infrastructure, to reduce latency and client-side flicker.

## 中文定义

抛弃前端 JS 分流，在 Vercel 或 Cloudflare 的 CDN 节点直接下发不同版本的 HTML，做到彻底的 0 闪烁。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Edge Experimentation helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Edge Experimentation into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
