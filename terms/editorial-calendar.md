# Editorial Calendar

## Definition

A planning system that schedules content topics, formats, owners, channels, and publication dates.

## 中文定义

编辑日历是记录内容主题、格式、负责人、渠道和发布时间的内容生产与发布计划系统。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply Editorial Calendar by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
