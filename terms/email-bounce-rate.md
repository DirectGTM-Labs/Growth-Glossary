# Email Bounce Rate

## Definition

The percentage of sent email messages rejected by recipient mail systems, usually separated into hard and soft bounces.

## 中文定义

邮件退信率是发出的邮件中被收件方邮件系统拒收的比例，通常进一步区分硬退信和软退信。

## Formula

Email Bounce Rate = Bounced Messages / Sent Messages

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Email Bounce Rate in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
