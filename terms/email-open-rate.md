# Email Open Rate

## Definition

The proportion of delivered emails recorded as opened; privacy features can make this metric less reliable as a direct measure of human attention.

## 中文定义

邮件打开率是被记录为打开的邮件占已投递邮件的比例；由于隐私保护机制会自动加载像素，它不再等同于真实人工阅读率。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Email Open Rate in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
