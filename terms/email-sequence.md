# Email Sequence

## Definition

An email sequence is an ordered set of emails sent according to timing, triggers, or recipient behavior.

## 中文定义

一串连环套自动化代码。如果用户没点邮件A，系统明天发备用方案B；如果点了A，瞬间发射成单逼迫邮件C。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Email Sequence helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Email Sequence to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
