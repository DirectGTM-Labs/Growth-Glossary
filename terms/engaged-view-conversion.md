# Engaged-View Conversion

## Definition

A conversion credited after a user meaningfully engages with a video ad without a traditional click and then converts within the supported window.

## 中文定义

互动观看型转化是用户对视频广告进行了达到平台定义阈值的观看互动但未产生传统点击，随后在支持时间窗内完成的转化。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Engaged-View Conversion alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
