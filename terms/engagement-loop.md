# Engagement Loop

## Definition

A feedback loop in which user actions create prompts, content, rewards, or social interactions that encourage repeated engagement.

## 中文定义

参与循环是用户行为产生新的内容、反馈、奖励或社交互动，并进一步推动用户再次参与的自强化机制。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Engagement Loop by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
