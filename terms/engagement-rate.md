# Engagement Rate

## Definition

The proportion of audience exposure, reach, followers, or views that results in defined interactions; the denominator must be stated because platform definitions vary.

## 中文定义

互动率衡量内容曝光、触达、观看或粉丝中有多少产生了规定互动；由于不同平台分母不同，使用时必须明确计算口径。

## Formula

Engagement Rate = Defined Engagements / Chosen Exposure Denominator (for example reach, impressions, views, or followers)

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply Engagement Rate by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
