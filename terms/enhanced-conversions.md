# Enhanced Conversions

## Definition

A Google Ads measurement feature that supplements conversion tags or uploaded conversions with consented, hashed first-party customer data to improve matching and measurement.

## 中文定义

增强型转化是 Google Ads 的测量能力，通过经过同意并哈希处理的第一方客户数据补充转化标签或上传数据，以改善匹配和转化测量。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Enhanced Conversions alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
