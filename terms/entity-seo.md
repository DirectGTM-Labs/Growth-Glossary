# Entity SEO

## Definition

Entity SEO focuses on making a business, person, product, place, or other entity clearly identifiable and consistently represented across search and knowledge systems.

## 中文定义

实体 SEO。别再堆词了，要把自己包装成 Google 知识图谱里的真凭实据的公司实体节点。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Entity SEO helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Entity SEO to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
