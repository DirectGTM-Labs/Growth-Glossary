# ETL

## Definition

ETL is a data integration pattern in which data is extracted from sources, transformed into a target structure, and loaded into a destination system.

## 中文定义

提取、转换、加载。把业务库里又脏又乱的数据，清洗打包放进数据仓库的数据管道工程。

## Why It Matters

Understanding ETL helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate ETL into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
