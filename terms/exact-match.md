# Exact Match

## Definition

A Google Ads keyword match type that targets searches with the same meaning or intent as the keyword, while still allowing supported close variants.

## 中文定义

完全匹配是 Google Ads 中针对与关键词具有相同含义或意图的搜索进行匹配的方式，同时仍可包含平台支持的近似变体。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Exact Match alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
