# Exit Intent

## Definition

A behavioral signal inferred when a user appears likely to leave a page or experience, sometimes used to trigger a last-moment intervention.

## 中文定义

退出意图是通过鼠标、滚动、返回操作等行为推断用户可能即将离开页面的信号，可用于触发最后阶段干预。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Exit Intent to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
