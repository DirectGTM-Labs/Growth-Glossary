# Expansion MRR

## Definition

Additional monthly recurring revenue generated from existing customers through upgrades, added seats, usage growth, or cross-sell.

## 中文定义

扩展 MRR 是现有客户通过升级、增加席位、增加用量或交叉销售带来的新增月度经常性收入。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Expansion MRR in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
