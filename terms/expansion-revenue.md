# Expansion Revenue

## Definition

Expansion revenue is additional revenue from existing customers through upgrades, additional seats, usage growth, or other expansion mechanisms.

## 中文定义

老客户因为爽，把原先 10 人的套餐加买到了 100 人，多收的那份钱，这是利润率最高的钱。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Expansion Revenue helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Expansion Revenue to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
