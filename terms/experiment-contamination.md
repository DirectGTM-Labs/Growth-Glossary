# Experiment Contamination

## Definition

A condition in which units receive elements of another treatment, communicate across groups, or otherwise violate the intended separation between experimental conditions.

## 中文定义

实验污染是对照组和实验组的边界被破坏，例如对象接触了其他处理、跨组传播信息或共享环境，从而削弱因果解释。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Experiment Contamination in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
