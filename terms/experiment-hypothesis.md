# Experiment Hypothesis

## Definition

A falsifiable statement predicting how a specific intervention will affect a defined metric for a defined population and why.

## 中文定义

实验假设是一条可证伪的预测：明确某项干预将如何影响某一人群的某个指标，并给出背后的因果理由。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Experiment Hypothesis in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
