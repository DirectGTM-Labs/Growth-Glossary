# Experiment Layer

## Definition

A namespace or traffic layer used to coordinate overlapping experiments, often so mutually exclusive tests do not allocate the same unit to conflicting treatments.

## 中文定义

实验层是用于协调并发实验的流量或命名空间机制，常用于保证指定实验之间互斥或避免处理冲突。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Experiment Layer in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
