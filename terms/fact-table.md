# Fact Table

## Definition

A fact table stores measurable business events or observations, usually with foreign keys to dimensions and numeric measures such as revenue or quantity.

## 中文定义

存储可度量的业务事件（如购买记录）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Fact Table helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
