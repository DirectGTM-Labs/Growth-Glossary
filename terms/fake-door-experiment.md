# Fake Door Experiment

## Definition

A fake door experiment tests demand for a proposed capability by presenting a credible entry point before the capability is actually available, measuring qualified intent while clearly communicating the user's next step.

## 中文定义

假门实验通过在功能尚未真正提供前展示一个可信的功能入口来测试需求，并测量有价值的意向；实施时应明确告知用户后续状态，避免欺骗。

## Why It Matters

It can test demand before committing substantial engineering resources, especially when paired with a clear disclosure or waitlist flow.

## Example

A product displays a clearly labeled upcoming feature and records qualified clicks and waitlist signups before building the full capability.
