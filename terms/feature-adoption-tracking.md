# Feature Adoption Tracking

## Definition

Feature adoption tracking measures which users or accounts use a product capability, how frequently, and how adoption changes over time.

## 中文定义

一种分析专门看：新功能发布 30 天了，到底有几个人真的用过哪怕一次。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Feature Adoption Tracking helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

For example, a growth team could incorporate Feature Adoption Tracking into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
