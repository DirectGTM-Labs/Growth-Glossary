# Feature Flags / Feature Toggles

## Definition

Feature flags are mechanisms that control whether a software capability is enabled for particular environments, users, segments, or traffic percentages, independently of the underlying deployment lifecycle.

## 中文定义

特性开关（Feature Flags / Feature Toggles）是一种控制机制，可以让某项软件能力按环境、用户、群体或流量比例启用或关闭，并将功能发布与代码部署生命周期解耦。

## Why It Matters

They enable controlled rollout, experimentation, rapid rollback, and risk management.

## Example

A new onboarding flow is enabled for 5% of eligible users while the remaining users continue to see the control experience.
