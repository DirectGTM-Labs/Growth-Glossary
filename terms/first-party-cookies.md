# First-party Cookies

## Definition

First-party cookies are browser cookies set in the context of the site the user is visiting, typically used for session state, preferences, or first-party measurement.

## Why It Matters

Understanding First-party Cookies helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
