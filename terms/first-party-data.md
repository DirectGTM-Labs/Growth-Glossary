# First-Party Data

## Definition

First-party data is information a company collects directly through its own interactions with customers or users, subject to applicable privacy and consent requirements.

## 中文定义

谁也剥夺不走的核心资产。你自己合法收集、存在自家数仓里被保护的极度干净的用户偏好和联系表。

## Why It Matters

Understanding First-Party Data helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use First-Party Data to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
