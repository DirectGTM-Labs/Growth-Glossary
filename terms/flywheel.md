# Flywheel

## Definition

A flywheel is a strategic model in which mutually reinforcing activities increase the momentum of a business system over time.

## 中文定义

亚马逊的发明，各环节互相提供动能（商品多->体验好->人多->卖家多->商品更多），前期极难推动，一旦转起来势不可挡。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Flywheel helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Flywheel to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
