# Fogg Behavior Model

## Definition

The Fogg Behavior Model proposes that behavior occurs when Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt converge at the same moment.

## 中文定义

行为发生需要动机、能力与触发。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Fogg Behavior Model helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Fogg Behavior Model to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
