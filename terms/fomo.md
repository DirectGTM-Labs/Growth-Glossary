# FOMO

## Definition

Fear of Missing Out (FOMO) is anxiety about missing a valuable, scarce, or socially important opportunity and can influence attention and purchase behavior.

## 中文定义

“仅剩最后 2 个名额”、“某某刚刚购买了”。制造焦虑迫使用户切断理智、立即下单。

## Why It Matters

Understanding FOMO helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use FOMO to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
