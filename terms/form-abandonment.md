# Form Abandonment

## Definition

A user beginning a form but leaving before successful submission.

## 中文定义

表单放弃是用户已经开始填写表单，但在成功提交之前离开的行为。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Form Abandonment to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
