# Four Fits

## Definition

Four Fits is a framework that examines the fit among product, market, channel, and business model rather than treating product-market fit as the only relevant alignment.

## 中文定义

只证明产品好没用，要想成事，你的产品、你切入的市场、你买量的渠道、你的定价模型四者必须齿轮般互相咬合。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Four Fits helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Four Fits to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
