# Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate

## Definition

The percentage of free users who convert to a paid plan within a defined observation window.

## 中文定义

免费转付费率是符合统计条件的免费用户中，在规定时间窗内升级成为付费用户的比例。

## Formula

Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate = Free Users Converted to Paid / Eligible Free Users

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
