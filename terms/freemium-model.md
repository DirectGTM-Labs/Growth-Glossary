# Freemium Model

## Definition

A freemium model offers a usable free tier while charging for additional features, capacity, service levels, or other premium value.

## 中文定义

免费增值模型通过提供可用的免费版本吸引和服务用户，并对高级功能、容量、服务等级或其他增值内容收费。

## Why It Matters

It can reduce initial adoption friction while creating a conversion path to paid plans.

## Example

A collaboration product offers free access for small teams and charges for advanced administration and larger usage limits.
