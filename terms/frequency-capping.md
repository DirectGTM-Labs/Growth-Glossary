# Frequency Capping

## Definition

A rule that limits how many messages or ad exposures an individual user can receive within a defined time window.

## 中文定义

频次控制是在规定时间窗口内限制单个用户能够接收多少条消息或看到多少次广告的规则。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Frequency Capping in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
