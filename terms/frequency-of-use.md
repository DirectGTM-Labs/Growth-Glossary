# Frequency of Use

## Definition

How often a user or account performs a defined meaningful product action within a time interval.

## 中文定义

使用频率表示用户或账户在规定时间内完成某项有意义产品行为的次数或周期。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Frequency of Use by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
