# Gabor-Granger Pricing Method

## Definition

A survey method that tests purchase likelihood at multiple proposed price points to estimate demand and revenue relationships.

## 中文定义

Gabor-Granger 定价法通过向受访者测试多个候选价格下的购买意愿，估算价格变化与需求、收入之间的关系。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Gabor-Granger Pricing Method when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
