# Google Click Identifier (GCLID)

## Definition

A parameter used by Google Ads to identify an ad click so downstream conversions can be attributed back to that click when measurement conditions are met.

## 中文定义

GCLID 是 Google Ads 用于标识一次广告点击的参数，在满足测量条件时可以把后续转化重新关联到该广告点击。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Google Click Identifier (GCLID) alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
