# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

## Definition

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is an emerging practice focused on improving the likelihood that information about a brand, organization, product, or topic is accurately retrieved, represented, and cited or surfaced by generative AI systems and answer engines.

## 中文定义

生成式引擎优化（GEO）是一种正在形成中的实践，重点是提高品牌、组织、产品或主题的信息被生成式 AI 系统和答案引擎准确检索、表示、引用或呈现的可能性。

## Why It Matters

As users increasingly obtain answers through AI-mediated interfaces, discoverability may depend on entity clarity, source quality, content structure, and the retrieval behavior of individual systems.

## Example

A company publishes clear, authoritative product documentation with consistent entity information and measures how answer engines represent and cite it.
