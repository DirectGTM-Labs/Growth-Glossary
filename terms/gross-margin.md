# Gross Margin

## Definition

The percentage of revenue remaining after cost of goods sold or cost of revenue.

## 中文定义

毛利率是收入扣除销售成本或收入成本后剩余毛利占收入的比例。

## Formula

Gross Margin = (Revenue − Cost of Revenue) / Revenue

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Gross Margin in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
