# Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)

## Definition

The total value of goods or services transacted through a marketplace before deductions such as refunds, fees, or platform commissions, according to the business definition.

## 中文定义

商品交易总额是平台在一定时期内促成的商品或服务交易总价值，通常是在退款、平台佣金等扣减之前的交易规模指标，具体口径应由业务明确。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
