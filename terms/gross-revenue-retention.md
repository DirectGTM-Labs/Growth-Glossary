# Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)

## Definition

The percentage of starting recurring revenue retained from existing customers after churn and contraction, excluding expansion.

## 中文定义

总收入留存率衡量期初现有客户经常性收入在扣除流失和降级后仍保留下来的比例，不计入扩张收入。

## Formula

GRR = (Starting Recurring Revenue − Churned Revenue − Contraction Revenue) / Starting Recurring Revenue

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
