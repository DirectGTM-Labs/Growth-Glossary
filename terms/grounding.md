# Grounding

## Definition

The process of constraining or supporting a generated answer with retrieved, tool-provided, or otherwise verifiable external information.

## 中文定义

事实依据绑定是用检索结果、工具返回结果或其他可验证外部信息约束和支持生成式答案的过程。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate Grounding across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
