# Growth Accounting

## Definition

A framework for decomposing user-base change into new, retained, churned, and resurrected users so teams can understand the sources of net growth.

## 中文定义

增长核算是一种把用户基数变化拆解为新增、留存、流失和复活等组成部分的方法，用于判断净增长究竟来自哪里。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Growth Accounting by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
