# Growth Engineering

## Definition

Growth Engineering is an interdisciplinary practice that applies software engineering, experimentation, analytics, and product thinking to systematically improve measurable growth outcomes across the customer or user lifecycle.

## 中文定义

增长工程是一种跨学科实践，将软件工程、实验、数据分析与产品思维结合起来，以系统化、可测量的方式改善用户或客户生命周期中的增长结果。

## Why It Matters

It connects product changes with measurement and experimentation, making growth work reproducible rather than relying only on intuition.

## Example

A team instruments onboarding events, launches a controlled experiment on the activation flow, and evaluates the effect on an agreed activation metric.
