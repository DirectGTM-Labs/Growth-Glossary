# Growth Loops

## Definition

A growth loop is a causal cycle in which an input passes through a product or business mechanism and produces an output that becomes an input for another iteration of the cycle, generating additional growth.

## 中文定义

增长循环是一种因果闭环：某个输入经过产品或业务机制产生输出，而该输出又成为下一轮循环的输入，从而持续产生增长。

## Why It Matters

Loops emphasize compounding mechanisms and causal feedback instead of treating growth as only a one-way funnel.

## Example

Users create public content → content is indexed and discovered → new visitors arrive → some visitors create more content.
