# Growth Model

## Definition

A structured representation of the mechanisms, inputs, constraints, and feedback loops that explain how a product or business acquires, retains, and monetizes customers over time.

## 中文定义

增长模型是对业务如何持续获得、激活、留存并变现用户的机制化描述，通常会明确关键输入、约束条件、反馈回路以及各变量之间的关系。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Growth Model by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
