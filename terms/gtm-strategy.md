# GTM Strategy

## Definition

A go-to-market (GTM) strategy defines how a company will reach a target market, create demand, convert buyers, deliver value, and scale distribution for a product or offer.

## Why It Matters

Understanding GTM Strategy helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use GTM Strategy to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
