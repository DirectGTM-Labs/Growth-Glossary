# Guardrail Metric

## Definition

A guardrail metric is a safety metric monitored during an experiment to detect unacceptable deterioration in performance, reliability, user experience, or business outcomes.

## 中文定义

为防短视设定的不可逾越的红线指标。比如测提高客单价，但“网页崩溃率”是护栏，崩了就得直接叫停。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Guardrail Metric helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Guardrail Metric into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
