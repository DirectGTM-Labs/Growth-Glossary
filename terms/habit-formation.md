# Habit Formation

## Definition

Habit formation describes the process by which repeated behavior becomes more automatic through repeated cues, actions, rewards, and contextual reinforcement. Product design can influence the conditions around repeated use but cannot guarantee a habit.

## 中文定义

习惯养成是指行为在重复的线索、行动、奖励和情境强化作用下逐渐变得更自动化的过程。产品设计可以影响重复使用的条件，但不能保证用户一定形成习惯。

## Why It Matters

Understanding repeated-use behavior can help teams design retention mechanisms without confusing engagement with genuine user value.

## Example

A language-learning product uses reminders and progress feedback to support repeated study sessions.
