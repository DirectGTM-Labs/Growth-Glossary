# Heatmap

## Definition

A visual aggregation of user interaction intensity, such as clicks, taps, pointer activity, or attention proxies, across a page or interface.

## 中文定义

热图是把页面上的点击、触摸、指针活动或其他互动强度进行聚合可视化的分析方式。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Heatmap to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
