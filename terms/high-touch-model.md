# High-touch Model

## Definition

A high-touch model relies on substantial human interaction such as sales, implementation, consulting, or customer-success engagement.

## 中文定义

高触式。恨不得把实施顾问塞进客户办公室里手把手辅导几周重工服务。

## Why It Matters

Understanding High-touch Model helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use High-touch Model to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
