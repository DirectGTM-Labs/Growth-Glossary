# Holdout Group

## Definition

A holdout group is a deliberately withheld population that does not receive a treatment or feature, providing a comparison for estimating longer-term or incremental effects.

## 中文定义

强制留下 5% 用户永远停留在极简旧版本，过半年拉出来一比，看看那群狂写几百个新功能的研发到底创造了多少真实增量。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Holdout Group helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
