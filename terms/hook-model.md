# Hook Model

## Definition

The Hook Model is a behavioral design framework consisting of Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, and Investment, used to analyze repeated product engagement.

## 中文定义

让人上瘾的四级台阶：被弹窗触发（Trigger）、花极低成本行动（Action）、被变幻莫测的内容惊艳（Variable Reward）、留下点赞或评论（Investment）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Hook Model helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Hook Model to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
