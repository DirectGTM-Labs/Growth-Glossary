# Hybrid Pricing

## Definition

A model that combines two or more charging mechanisms, such as a platform fee plus seats or included usage plus overage.

## 中文定义

混合定价把两种或多种收费机制组合使用，例如固定平台费加席位费，或基础额度加超额用量费。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Hybrid Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
