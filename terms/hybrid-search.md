# Hybrid Search

## Definition

A retrieval approach that combines lexical keyword matching with semantic or vector-based retrieval to improve recall and relevance.

## 中文定义

混合搜索是把关键词型词法检索与向量或语义检索结合起来，以同时改善召回率和相关性的检索方式。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate Hybrid Search across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
