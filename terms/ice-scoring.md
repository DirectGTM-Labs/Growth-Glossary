# ICE Scoring

## Definition

ICE scoring prioritizes ideas using Impact, Confidence, and Ease, usually as a lightweight comparative heuristic.

## 中文定义

为成百上千的点子排队计分的简易方法：这个改动有多大影响力（I）、你有几成把握能成（C）、开发哥哥要写几天代码（E）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding ICE Scoring helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use ICE Scoring to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
