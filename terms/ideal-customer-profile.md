# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Definition

An Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) defines the characteristics of organizations or accounts most likely to derive value and become economically attractive customers, especially in B2B.

## 中文定义

理想客户画像是与“Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)”对应的专业概念，用于描述该术语所代表的业务、工程、数据、实验或增长机制。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
