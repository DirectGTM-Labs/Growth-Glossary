# Identity Graph

## Definition

A data structure that links multiple identifiers, devices, accounts, and profiles believed to represent the same person or organization.

## 中文定义

身份图谱是一种把多个设备、匿名 ID、登录 ID、账户和其他标识连接起来，以表示同一人或同一组织的数据结构。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Identity Graph in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
