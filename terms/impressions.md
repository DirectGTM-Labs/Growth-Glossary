# Impressions

## Definition

The total number of times content or an advertisement is displayed or served, including repeated exposures to the same user.

## 中文定义

展示次数是内容或广告被展示的总次数，同一用户重复看到会产生多次展示。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply Impressions by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
