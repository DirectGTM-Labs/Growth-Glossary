# In-App Messaging

## Definition

In-app messaging delivers contextual messages inside a product interface to guide, educate, notify, or prompt users.

## 中文定义

应用内插屏。刚滑到一个特定页面，恰好弹出一张“正中你当下所需”的小贴心卡片。

## Why It Matters

Understanding In-App Messaging helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use In-App Messaging to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
