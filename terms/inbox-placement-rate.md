# Inbox Placement Rate

## Definition

The proportion of sent or delivered email that reaches the primary inbox rather than spam, junk, or other filtered folders.

## 中文定义

收件箱落地率是邮件真正进入主收件箱而不是垃圾邮件、促销过滤或其他文件夹的比例。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Inbox Placement Rate in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
