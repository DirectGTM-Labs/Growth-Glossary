# Incrementality Testing

## Definition

Incrementality testing estimates causal lift by comparing outcomes under an intervention with a valid counterfactual, often through randomized or quasi-experimental designs.

## 中文定义

拔插销验证法。强制断停一个月最大的广告源泉，大盘销量如果纹丝没断血说明前置全是骗量被大盘本身吸走的。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Incrementality Testing helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Incrementality Testing to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
