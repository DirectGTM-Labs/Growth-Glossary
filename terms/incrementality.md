# Incrementality

## Definition

Incrementality is the causal lift attributable to an intervention beyond what would have happened without the intervention.

## 中文定义

广告带来的“净多出”的订单。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Incrementality helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Incrementality to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
