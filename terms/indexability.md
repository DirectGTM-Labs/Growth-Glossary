# Indexability

## Definition

The extent to which a crawled page is eligible to be stored and served by a search engine, given directives, canonicalization, quality, and technical constraints.

## 中文定义

可索引性表示某个已抓取页面在索引指令、规范化、技术条件和内容质量等因素下，是否具备进入搜索索引的资格。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Indexability during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
