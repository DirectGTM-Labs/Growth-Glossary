# Influencer Marketing

## Definition

Influencer marketing uses creators or trusted individuals to communicate with audiences on behalf of a brand, product, or campaign.

## 中文定义

## Why It Matters

Understanding Influencer Marketing helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Influencer Marketing to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
