# Information Gain

## Definition

Information gain is the reduction in uncertainty produced by new information; in content strategy, the phrase can also describe substantive information or evidence added beyond existing coverage.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Information Gain helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Information Gain to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
