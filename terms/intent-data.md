# Intent Data

## Definition

Intent data contains signals that suggest a person or organization is researching, considering, or becoming more likely to purchase a product or solution.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Intent Data helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Intent Data to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
