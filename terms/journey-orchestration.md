# Customer Journey Orchestration

## Definition

The real-time coordination of messages, decisions, channels, and experiences based on customer data and behavior across a journey.

## 中文定义

客户旅程编排是依据实时客户数据和行为，在不同阶段协调消息、渠道、决策逻辑和体验的过程。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Customer Journey Orchestration in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
