# K-Factor

## Definition

The K-factor is a viral-growth metric typically modeled as the number of invitations or referrals generated per user multiplied by the conversion rate of those referrals.

## 中文定义

一个老用户能带来几个新用户的拉新比例。

## Why It Matters

Understanding K-Factor helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use K-Factor to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
