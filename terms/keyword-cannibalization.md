# Keyword Cannibalization

## Definition

An SEO diagnosis in which multiple pages from the same site compete for similar queries or intents in a way that may dilute clarity, links, or ranking performance.

## 中文定义

关键词蚕食是同一网站多个页面针对高度相似的查询或意图竞争，可能导致主题边界、内部链接和排名信号被分散的情况。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Keyword Cannibalization during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
