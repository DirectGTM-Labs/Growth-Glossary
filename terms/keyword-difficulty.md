# Keyword Difficulty

## Definition

Keyword difficulty is a third-party or platform-specific estimate of how difficult it may be to rank for a search query, usually based on competition and authority signals.

## 中文定义

关键字难度。“二手车”的难度是修罗级，根本做不上去；但“北京带牌事故二手车代办”就极度容易。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Keyword Difficulty helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Keyword Difficulty to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
