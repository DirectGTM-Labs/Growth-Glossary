# Knowledge Graph

## Definition

A knowledge graph represents entities and relationships as a structured graph to support search, retrieval, and reasoning.

将世间万物以“实体-关系”连接起来的网络，GEO 优化的核心就是让自己成为图谱上的关键实体。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Knowledge Graph helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Knowledge Graph to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
