# Kubernetes

## Definition

Kubernetes is a container orchestration platform for deploying, scaling, networking, and managing containerized workloads.

## 中文定义

自动管理成千上万个 Docker 集装箱的自动化编排系统。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Kubernetes helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

A growth engineering team can use Kubernetes as part of a production workflow where reliability, observability, security, and measurable outcomes are defined explicitly.
