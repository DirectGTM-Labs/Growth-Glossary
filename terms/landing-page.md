# Landing Page

## Definition

A page designed around a specific campaign, audience, or conversion objective, typically with a focused value proposition and call to action.

## 中文定义

落地页是针对特定渠道、受众或转化目标设计的页面，通常强调清晰价值主张和单一或有限的行动号召。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Landing Page to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
