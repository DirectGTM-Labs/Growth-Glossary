# Lead Generation

## Definition

The process of acquiring identifiable prospects who have expressed some level of interest and can be nurtured or qualified.

## 中文定义

线索生成是获取可识别潜在客户信息，并形成后续可以培养、评分或销售跟进的线索池的过程。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Lead Generation in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
