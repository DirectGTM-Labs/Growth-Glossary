# Lifecycle Marketing

## Definition

A strategy for coordinating communications and experiences according to a customer's stage, behavior, needs, and relationship with the brand.

## 中文定义

生命周期营销是根据客户所处阶段、行为、需求和与品牌的关系，持续协调不同沟通和体验的营销策略。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Lifecycle Marketing in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
