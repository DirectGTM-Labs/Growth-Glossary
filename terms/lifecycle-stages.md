# Lifecycle Stages

## Definition

Lifecycle stages classify customers or leads by their relationship to the business, such as prospect, activated user, customer, retained customer, or churned customer.

## 中文定义

阶段标刻。把进来的人打上冷血的机器标签：“纯游客”、“有一点点想买的游客”、“核心大款”、“准备明天退款大款”。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Lifecycle Stages helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Lifecycle Stages to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
