# Lifetime Value (LTV)

## Definition

Lifetime Value (LTV) is an estimate of the economic value generated by a customer or user over the relevant relationship or observation horizon. The exact calculation depends on the business model and whether revenue, gross profit, or contribution margin is being measured.

## 中文定义

用户生命周期价值（LTV）是对客户或用户在相关关系周期或观察期间所产生经济价值的估计。具体计算取决于商业模式，以及采用收入、毛利还是贡献利润作为价值口径。

## Formula

A simplified form is LTV = Average Revenue per Customer per Period × Expected Customer Lifetime. More rigorous models use contribution margin, retention/churn, discounting, and cohort behavior.

## Why It Matters

LTV helps evaluate the long-term economics of acquisition, retention, pricing, and customer segments.

## Example

A subscription business estimates cohort LTV using observed retention and contribution margin rather than assuming every customer remains indefinitely.
