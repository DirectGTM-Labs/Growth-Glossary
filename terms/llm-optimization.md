# LLM Optimization (LLMO)

## Definition

An emerging industry term for practices intended to improve how information about an entity, product, or source is retrieved, represented, or cited by large-language-model systems; terminology and measurement remain non-standardized.

## 中文定义

大语言模型优化是一个新兴行业术语，通常指提升品牌、实体、产品或信息在 LLM 检索、表征和引用中的表现；目前其定义与衡量方式尚未标准化。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate LLM Optimization (LLMO) across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
