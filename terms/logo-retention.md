# Logo Retention

## Definition

The percentage of customer accounts retained over a period, measured by customer count rather than revenue.

## 中文定义

客户数留存率按客户账户数量而不是收入金额来衡量一段时间后仍被保留的客户比例。

## Formula

Logo Retention = Retained Customer Accounts / Starting Customer Accounts

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Logo Retention in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
