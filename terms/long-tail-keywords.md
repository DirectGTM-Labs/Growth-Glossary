# Long-tail Keywords

## Definition

Long-tail keywords are relatively specific search queries that often have lower individual search volume and can reflect narrower intent.

## 中文定义

诸如“2026年适合上海梅雨天带防臭垫的猫砂盆哪款最好”。搜的人少得可怜，但一旦搜了，基本当场掏腰包买。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Long-tail Keywords helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Long-tail Keywords to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
