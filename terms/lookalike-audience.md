# Lookalike Audience

## Definition

A lookalike audience is an audience modeled by an advertising platform to resemble a source audience according to platform-defined attributes or behavior.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Lookalike Audience helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
