# Loss Aversion

## Definition

Loss aversion is the behavioral tendency for losses to have greater psychological impact than comparable gains, under many decision contexts.

## 中文定义

人类丢掉 100 块钱的痛苦，远大于捡到 100 块钱的快乐。用“放弃试用将失去云端记录”来逼单的底层逻辑。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Loss Aversion helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Loss Aversion to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
