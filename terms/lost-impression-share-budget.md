# Search Lost Impression Share (Budget)

## Definition

The estimated percentage of eligible Search impressions lost because campaign budget was insufficient.

## 中文定义

因预算损失的搜索展示份额是由于广告系列预算不足而错失的估算可获得搜索展示比例。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Search Lost Impression Share (Budget) alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
