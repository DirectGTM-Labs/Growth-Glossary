# Search Lost Impression Share (Rank)

## Definition

The estimated percentage of eligible Search impressions lost because Ad Rank was not high enough.

## 中文定义

因排名损失的搜索展示份额是由于 Ad Rank 不足而错失的估算可获得搜索展示比例。

## Formula

Search Lost IS (Rank) = Estimated Eligible Impressions Lost Due to Rank / Estimated Eligible Impressions

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Search Lost Impression Share (Rank) alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, landing-page experience, or ad quality.
