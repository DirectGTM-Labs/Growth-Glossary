# Low-touch Model

## Definition

A low-touch model serves many customers with standardized, automated, or lightweight human interactions rather than intensive account-by-account support.

## 中文定义

轻触式。几乎没客服、靠极佳界面全自动运转的极轻型业务流。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Low-touch Model helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Low-touch Model to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
