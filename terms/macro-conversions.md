# Macro-conversions

## Definition

Macro-conversions are the primary business outcomes a journey is designed to produce, such as a purchase, paid subscription, or qualified contract.

## 中文定义

巨集转化/终极变现。一切实验最终都是以是否在这交钱签收合同落下绝地反杀为收尾的。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Macro-conversions helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Macro-conversions to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
