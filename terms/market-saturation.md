# Market Saturation

## Definition

Market saturation describes a condition in which additional growth becomes harder because a large share of the relevant demand or customer base is already served.

## 中文定义

市场饱和度。全天下所有人都有这破手机了，连买量都买不到一个新人，必须卷存量了。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Market Saturation helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Market Saturation to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
