# Marketing Automation

## Definition

Marketing automation uses software to execute, coordinate, trigger, and measure marketing activities based on schedules, rules, user data, or behavior.

## 中文定义

营销自动化大熔炉系统（HubSpot）。只要用户满足“昨天登陆+看了特价页”，系统凌晨 1 点自动发短信并且打六折。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Marketing Automation helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Marketing Automation to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
