# Marketing Operations (MOps)

## Definition

The function responsible for marketing processes, technology, data, governance, measurement, and operational efficiency.

## 中文定义

营销运营负责营销流程、技术栈、数据、治理、衡量体系以及整体执行效率。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Marketing Operations (MOps) so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
