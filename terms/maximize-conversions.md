# Maximize Conversions

## Definition

A Google Ads bidding strategy that uses available budget and machine learning to maximize the number of selected conversions.

## 中文定义

尽可能提高转化次数是一种自动出价策略，使用可用预算和机器学习来尽量增加选定转化动作的数量。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Maximize Conversions alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
