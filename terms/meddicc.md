# MEDDICC

## Definition

A B2B sales qualification framework covering Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion, and Competition.

## 中文定义

MEDDICC 是用于复杂 B2B 销售资格判断的框架，核心维度包括量化价值、经济决策者、决策标准、决策流程、痛点、内部支持者和竞争情况。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include MEDDICC in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
