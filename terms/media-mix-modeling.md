# Media Mix Modeling (MMM)

## Definition

Media Mix Modeling (MMM) uses aggregated time-series or panel data and statistical models to estimate the contribution of marketing channels while avoiding dependence on individual-level attribution.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Media Mix Modeling (MMM) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
