# Metered Billing

## Definition

A billing method that calculates charges from recorded usage during a billing period.

## 中文定义

计量计费是在一个账单周期内记录客户实际使用量，并据此计算最终应付金额的计费方式。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Metered Billing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
