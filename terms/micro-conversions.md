# Micro-conversions

## Definition

Micro-conversions are intermediate actions that indicate progress toward a larger desired outcome, such as viewing pricing, starting a trial, or completing a key setup step.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Micro-conversions helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Micro-conversions to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
