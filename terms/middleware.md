# Middleware

## Definition

Middleware is software executed between stages of request or data processing, commonly used for cross-cutting concerns such as authentication, logging, and transformation.

## 中文定义

请求到达核心数据库前，必须要过的层层安检门（如查 Token 门、限流门）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Middleware helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
