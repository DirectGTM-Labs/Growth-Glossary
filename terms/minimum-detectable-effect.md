# Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE)

## Definition

The minimum detectable effect is the smallest effect size that an experiment is designed and powered to detect with specified statistical criteria, such as significance level and power.

## 中文定义

最小可检测效应（MDE）是实验在给定显著性水平、统计功效等设计条件下，希望能够可靠检测到的最小效应量。

## Formula

Required sample size is a function of MDE, baseline rate or variance, significance level, statistical power, and test design.

## Why It Matters

MDE links the business importance of an effect to the amount of traffic and observation time an experiment requires.

## Example

A team decides that a 5% relative lift is the smallest commercially meaningful effect and powers the experiment to detect it.
