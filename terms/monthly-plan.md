# Monthly Plan

## Definition

A recurring subscription term billed or renewed monthly.

## 中文定义

月度方案是按月计费或续订的订阅方案，通常提供更低的承诺门槛和更高的取消灵活性。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Monthly Plan when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
