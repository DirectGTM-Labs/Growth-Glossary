# Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

## Definition

The normalized recurring revenue generated from active subscriptions in a month, excluding one-time charges.

## 中文定义

月度经常性收入是有效订阅在一个月内可预期产生的标准化经常性收入，通常不包含一次性收费。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
