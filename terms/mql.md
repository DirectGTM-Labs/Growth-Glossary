# MQL

## Definition

A Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) is a lead that meets an organization’s defined marketing qualification criteria based on fit, engagement, intent, or related signals.

## Why It Matters

Understanding MQL helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use MQL to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
