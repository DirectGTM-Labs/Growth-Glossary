# Multi-Armed Bandit (MAB)

## Definition

A multi-armed bandit is a sequential decision framework that allocates observations among alternatives while balancing exploration of uncertain options and exploitation of options currently estimated to perform better.

## 中文定义

多臂老虎机（MAB）是一种序贯决策框架，在探索不确定选项与利用当前估计表现更好的选项之间进行流量或机会分配。

## Why It Matters

Bandit methods can be useful when continuous allocation decisions and opportunity cost matter, although they solve a different decision problem from a fixed-horizon causal A/B test.

## Example

An ad system dynamically allocates impressions among several creatives while learning which alternatives perform better.
