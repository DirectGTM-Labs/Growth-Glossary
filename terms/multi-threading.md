# Sales Multi-threading

## Definition

A strategy of building relationships with multiple stakeholders inside a target account to reduce single-contact risk and improve deal resilience.

## 中文定义

销售多线程是销售人员同时与一个目标账户中的多位关键利益相关者建立关系，以降低单一联系人风险并提高复杂交易韧性的策略。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Sales Multi-threading in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
