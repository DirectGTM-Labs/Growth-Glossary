# Multiple Testing

## Definition

The statistical problem that arises when many hypotheses or metrics are tested, increasing the chance of false positive findings unless the analysis accounts for multiplicity.

## 中文定义

多重检验问题是同时检验许多假设或指标时，偶然产生假阳性的概率会随检验数量增加，需要在分析中处理这种多重性。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Multiple Testing in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
