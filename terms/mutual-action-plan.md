# Mutual Action Plan (MAP)

## Definition

A shared plan between buyer and seller that documents milestones, responsibilities, dates, and decision steps required to complete a purchase.

## 中文定义

共同执行计划是买卖双方共享的一份成交推进计划，明确完成采购所需的里程碑、责任人、日期和决策步骤。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Mutual Action Plan (MAP) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
