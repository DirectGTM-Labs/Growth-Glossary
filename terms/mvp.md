# MVP

## Definition

A minimum viable product (MVP) is the smallest product or experimentable offering that can test a critical value or business hypothesis with real users.

## 中文定义

别憋大招做半年，花三天做一个丑陋但能验证核心假设的破烂功能丢出去看有没有人点。

## Why It Matters

Understanding MVP helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use MVP to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
