# N-Day Retention

## Definition

The percentage of users in a cohort who return and perform the retention event on a specific day N after their start date.

## 中文定义

N 日留存是某一同期群用户中，在起始日期后的第 N 天再次完成指定留存行为的用户比例。

## Formula

N-Day Retention = Users Active on Day N / Users in Starting Cohort

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track N-Day Retention by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
