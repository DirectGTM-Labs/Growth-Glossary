# Negative Churn

## Definition

Negative churn is an informal term for a state in which expansion from an existing customer cohort exceeds the revenue lost from churn and contraction, producing net expansion.

## 中文定义

增购收入超过流失收入的理想增长状态。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Negative Churn helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Negative Churn to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
