# Negative Keywords

## Definition

Terms used to prevent ads from serving for searches that match excluded meanings or phrases according to the platform's negative-match rules.

## 中文定义

否定关键词用于排除不希望触发广告的搜索需求，使广告在符合相应否定匹配规则的查询上不参与展示。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Negative Keywords alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
