# Net New MRR

## Definition

The net monthly recurring revenue added in a period after accounting for new, expansion, contraction, and churned MRR.

## 中文定义

净新增 MRR 是新客户 MRR 与扩展 MRR 之和，再减去收缩 MRR 和流失 MRR 后得到的净变化。

## Formula

Net New MRR = New MRR + Expansion MRR − Contraction MRR − Churned MRR

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Net New MRR in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
