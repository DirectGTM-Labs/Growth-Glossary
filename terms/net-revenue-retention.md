# Net Revenue Retention

## Definition

Net Revenue Retention (NRR) measures how recurring revenue from an existing customer cohort changes over time after accounting for expansion, contraction, and churn, excluding new customers.

## 中文定义

SaaS 公司估值的命脉。即使不拉任何一个新客，老客升级掏的钱如果大于流失亏的钱（NRR>100%），公司就在奇迹般自动增长。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Net Revenue Retention helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Net Revenue Retention to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
