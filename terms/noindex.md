# noindex

## Definition

A robots directive instructing supporting search engines not to include the crawled resource in search results.

## 中文定义

noindex 是一种机器人指令，用于要求支持该指令的搜索引擎不要把已抓取的资源纳入搜索结果索引。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit noindex during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
