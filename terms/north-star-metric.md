# North Star Metric (NSM)

## Definition

A North Star Metric is a high-level metric intended to represent the core value a product delivers to customers and to align teams around a meaningful growth outcome. It is not necessarily the company's only or ultimate business metric.

## 中文定义

北极星指标（NSM）是一个用于代表产品为客户创造的核心价值、并帮助团队围绕重要增长结果对齐的高层指标。它并不一定是公司的唯一或最终商业指标。

## Why It Matters

A well-designed NSM helps connect product work to customer value while avoiding optimization around isolated local metrics.

## Example

A collaboration product may use meaningful weekly collaborative activity as an NSM while also monitoring revenue and reliability as business and guardrail metrics.
