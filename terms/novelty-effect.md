# Novelty Effect

## Definition

A temporary behavioral response caused by the newness of a treatment rather than its durable value.

## 中文定义

新奇效应是用户因为新版本的新鲜感而暂时改变行为，但这种变化并不一定代表长期价值。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Novelty Effect in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
