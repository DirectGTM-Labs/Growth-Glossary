# NPS

## Definition

NPS is a concept, method, technology, metric, or practice used in Growth Hacking. In this glossary, it refers to the defined mechanism or operating idea represented by the term, with its scope and assumptions stated explicitly rather than treating the term as a universal rule. The source material associates it with: 让用户打出 1-10 分.

## 中文定义

让用户打出 1-10 分，拿死忠粉（9-10分）比例减去狂黑粉（1-6分）比例的硬派口碑分数。
