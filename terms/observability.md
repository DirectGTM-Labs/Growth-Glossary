# Observability

## Definition

Observability is the ability to infer the internal state and behavior of a system from the signals it produces, commonly including logs, metrics, and traces.

## 中文定义

可观测性是根据系统对外产生的信号推断系统内部状态和行为的能力，常见信号包括日志、指标和链路追踪。

## Why It Matters

Observability allows teams to diagnose system and user-experience problems using evidence from production systems.

## Example

An engineer correlates request traces, error logs, and latency metrics to locate a slow checkout dependency.
