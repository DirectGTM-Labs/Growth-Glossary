# Offline Conversion

## Definition

A conversion that occurs outside the immediate online ad interaction, such as a later CRM-qualified lead or in-store sale, and is imported or connected back to advertising data.

## 中文定义

离线转化是发生在即时在线广告互动之外、但可以通过 CRM 或其他数据连接回传并关联到广告触点的业务结果，例如后续成单。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Offline Conversion alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
