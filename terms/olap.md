# OLAP (Online Analytical Processing)

## Definition

OLAP systems are designed for analytical workloads involving large scans, aggregations, joins, and multidimensional analysis rather than high-frequency transactional updates.

## 中文定义

联机分析处理（OLAP）系统面向分析型工作负载，适合大规模扫描、聚合、连接和多维分析，而非高频事务更新。

## Why It Matters

Growth teams use OLAP systems to analyze cohorts, funnels, experiments, revenue, and behavioral data at scale.

## Example

An analyst queries a warehouse to compare 30-day retention across acquisition channels and signup cohorts.
