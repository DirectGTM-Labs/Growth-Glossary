# OLTP (Online Transaction Processing)

## Definition

OLTP systems support operational workloads characterized by frequent transactions, concurrent writes, point lookups, and strong consistency requirements.

## 中文定义

联机事务处理（OLTP）系统用于支持高频业务事务，通常具有大量并发写入、点查询和一致性要求。

## Why It Matters

Growth systems often depend on OLTP databases as the operational source of customer, order, account, and product state.

## Example

A PostgreSQL database records account creation, subscription changes, and orders as transactional operations.
