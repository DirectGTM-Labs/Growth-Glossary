# On-page SEO

## Definition

On-page SEO improves content, structure, internal linking, metadata, and page-level relevance signals to better satisfy search intent and retrieval.

## 中文定义

站内优化。在 H1 标题里埋词，在段落里重复关键概念等人类能看到的页面调整。

## Why It Matters

Understanding On-page SEO helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use On-page SEO to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
