# Onboarding Checklist

## Definition

A visible list of setup or activation tasks that helps new users progress through initial product adoption.

## 中文定义

新手引导清单把新用户需要完成的初始化或激活任务可视化列出，帮助用户逐步达到首次价值。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Onboarding Checklist to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
