# Onboarding Completion Rate

## Definition

The percentage of users who complete the defined onboarding flow or required setup sequence.

## 中文定义

新手引导完成率是开始进入新手引导流程的用户中，最终完成规定全部或核心步骤的比例。

## Formula

Onboarding Completion Rate = Users Completing Onboarding / Users Starting Onboarding

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Onboarding Completion Rate by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
