# Opportunity-to-Close Rate

## Definition

The percentage of qualified opportunities that progress to closed-won status.

## 中文定义

商机成交率是进入规定合格商机阶段的机会中，最终成为 Closed-Won 的比例。

## Formula

Opportunity-to-Close Rate = Closed-Won Opportunities / Qualified Opportunities

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Opportunity-to-Close Rate in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
