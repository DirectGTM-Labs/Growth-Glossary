# Opt-out Churn

## Definition

Opt-out churn occurs when a customer explicitly chooses to end a subscription or relationship rather than simply becoming inactive.

## 中文定义

这种人极其坚决，找到设置最深处，硬是点下了“老子不干了关闭自动续费”按键。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Opt-out Churn helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Opt-out Churn to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
