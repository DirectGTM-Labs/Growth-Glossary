# Organic Traffic

## Definition

Organic traffic is traffic attributed to unpaid search results or another defined non-paid discovery channel, depending on the analytics taxonomy.

## 中文定义

你在 Google 或小红书凭极其扎实的内容硬实力，在不给平台交保护费的情况下源源不断吸来的用户群。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Organic Traffic helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Organic Traffic to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
