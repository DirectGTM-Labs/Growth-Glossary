# Orphan Page

## Definition

A page that has no crawlable internal links pointing to it from the site's normal linked structure.

## 中文定义

孤儿页面是在网站常规可抓取内部链接结构中，没有任何其他页面链接指向它的页面。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Orphan Page during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
