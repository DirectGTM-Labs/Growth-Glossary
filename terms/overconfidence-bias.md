# Overconfidence Bias

## Definition

Overconfidence bias is a tendency to overestimate the accuracy of one's knowledge, judgments, or predictions.

## 中文定义

过度自信偏差是高估自身知识、判断或预测准确性的认知偏差。

## Why It Matters

In experimentation, overconfidence can lead teams to ship or reject ideas based on intuition before collecting reliable evidence.

## Example

A team predicts a new CTA will increase conversion by 20% without a test, then uses an experiment to evaluate that assumption.
