# p-value

## Definition

A p-value is the probability, under the specified null hypothesis and statistical model, of observing a test statistic at least as extreme as the one obtained, in the relevant direction or definition of extremeness.

## 中文定义

p 值是在指定原假设和统计模型成立的条件下，观察到当前检验统计量或更极端结果的概率。

## Why It Matters

P-values quantify evidence against a null hypothesis under a specified model, but they do not represent the probability that the null hypothesis is true.

## Example

A pre-specified hypothesis test returns p = 0.03; this is evidence against the null under the chosen test, not a statement that there is a 97% probability the treatment is correct.
