# Product Packaging

## Definition

The design of which features, limits, services, and entitlements are bundled into each commercial offer or plan.

## 中文定义

产品打包是把功能、使用额度、服务和权益组合成不同商业套餐的设计过程。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Product Packaging when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
