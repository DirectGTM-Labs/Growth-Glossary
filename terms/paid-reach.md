# Paid Reach

## Definition

The number of unique users reached through paid media distribution.

## 中文定义

付费触达是通过广告预算和付费媒体分发获得的去重用户触达数量。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply Paid Reach by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
