# Partner-led Growth

## Definition

Partner-led growth uses resellers, integrators, affiliates, technology partners, agencies, or other external organizations as distribution and growth channels.

## 中文定义

代理渠道驱动。靠无数家像毛细血管一样分散的三方公司帮你分发并吃极其肥厚的抽水模式。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Partner-led Growth helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Partner-led Growth to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
