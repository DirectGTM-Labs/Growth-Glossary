# Payback Period

## Definition

Payback period is the time required for cumulative contribution or gross profit attributable to a customer or investment to recover the initial acquisition or investment cost.

## 中文定义

把花出去的 CAC 赚回来所需的时间。对于 SaaS，如果超过 12 个月回本，公司很容易因为现金流断裂而暴毙。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Payback Period helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Payback Period to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
