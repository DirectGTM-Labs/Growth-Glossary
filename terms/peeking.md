# Peeking

## Definition

The repeated inspection of conventional fixed-horizon test results before the planned stopping point, which can inflate false-positive risk when the statistical method does not support sequential monitoring.

## 中文定义

实验偷看是反复查看传统固定样本量检验的中间结果并提前决定是否停止；如果方法未支持序贯监控，会提高假阳性风险。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Peeking in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
