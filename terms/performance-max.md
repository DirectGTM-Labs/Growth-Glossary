# Performance Max

## Definition

A goal-based Google Ads campaign type that can access multiple Google advertising inventories and uses automation for bidding, targeting, and creative combinations.

## 中文定义

Performance Max 是 Google Ads 的目标导向型广告系列类型，可使用多个 Google 广告资源位，并依赖自动化完成出价、受众扩展和素材组合。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Performance Max alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
