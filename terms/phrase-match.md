# Phrase Match

## Definition

A Google Ads keyword match type that can match searches including the keyword's meaning, generally offering narrower reach than broad match and broader reach than exact match.

## 中文定义

词组匹配是 Google Ads 的关键词匹配方式，可以匹配包含关键词含义的搜索，其覆盖范围通常介于广泛匹配和完全匹配之间。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Phrase Match alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
