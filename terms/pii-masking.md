# PII Masking

## Definition

PII masking is the transformation or redaction of personally identifiable information so that systems can operate on data with reduced exposure of sensitive identifiers.

## 中文定义

在数据写入数据库或发送给第三方前，在内存里用 SHA-256 算法把邮箱/手机号加密成不可逆乱码的合规动作。
