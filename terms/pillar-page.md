# Pillar Page

## Definition

A broad, authoritative hub page that covers a core topic and links to more specific supporting content.

## 中文定义

支柱页面是覆盖一个核心主题、承担内容中心角色并链接到多个更细分支持页面的综合型页面。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Pillar Page during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
