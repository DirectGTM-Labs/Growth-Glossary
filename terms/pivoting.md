# Pivoting

## Definition

Pivoting is a substantive change in a startup or product strategy while retaining some validated learning, assets, or capabilities from the prior direction.

## 中文定义

撞到南墙后，保留公司的一部分核心资产（如团队/某技术），180度大掉头去干完全不同的赛道。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Pivoting helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Pivoting to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
