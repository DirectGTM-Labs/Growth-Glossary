# Power Analysis

## Definition

Power analysis evaluates the relationship among sample size, effect size, variability, significance level, and statistical power, commonly before an experiment.

Understanding Power Analysis helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
