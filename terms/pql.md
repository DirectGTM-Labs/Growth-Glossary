# PQL

## Definition

A Product Qualified Lead (PQL) is a lead whose product usage or product-derived signals meet defined criteria indicating a higher likelihood of commercial conversion or sales readiness.

## Why It Matters

Understanding PQL helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use PQL to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
