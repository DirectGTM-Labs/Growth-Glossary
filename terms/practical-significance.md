# Practical Significance

## Definition

The degree to which an observed effect is large enough to matter for users, business outcomes, cost, risk, or strategy, regardless of statistical significance alone.

## 中文定义

实际显著性关注实验效应是否大到足以对用户、收入、成本、风险或战略产生实际价值，而不仅仅是统计上是否显著。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Practical Significance in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
