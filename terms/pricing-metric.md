# Pricing Metric

## Definition

The unit used to calculate how much a customer pays, such as seats, transactions, contacts, data volume, or usage.

## 中文定义

计价指标是实际决定客户需要支付多少钱的计费单位，例如席位数、交易数、联系人数量或数据用量。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Pricing Metric when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
