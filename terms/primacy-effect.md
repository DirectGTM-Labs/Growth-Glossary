# Primacy Effect

## Definition

In experimentation, a temporary response in which users initially react negatively or differently because they are accustomed to the prior experience.

## 中文定义

首因效应在产品实验中常指用户因为习惯旧体验，对新版本最初产生暂时性不适或异常反应，之后可能逐渐恢复。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Primacy Effect in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
