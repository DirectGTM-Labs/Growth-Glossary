# Primary Conversion

## Definition

A conversion action designated for primary reporting and, where supported, bidding optimization.

## 中文定义

主要转化是被指定用于核心转化报告，并在支持的情况下参与自动出价优化的转化操作。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Primary Conversion alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
