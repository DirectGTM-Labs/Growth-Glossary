# Privacy-first Marketing

## Definition

Privacy-first marketing designs measurement, personalization, and targeting around data minimization, user rights, lawful processing, consent where required, and privacy-preserving alternatives.

## 中文定义

全盘隐私合规大考。在不允许获取任何个人终端机密设备号的绝境下，仅凭模糊的群体概率沙盒来做巨量营销核算的最高技术门槛。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Privacy-first Marketing helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Privacy-first Marketing to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
