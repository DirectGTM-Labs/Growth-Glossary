# Product-Led Growth (PLG)

## Definition

Product-Led Growth (PLG) is a growth model in which the product itself is a primary driver of user acquisition, activation, retention, expansion, or monetization, often supported by self-serve experiences.

## 中文定义

产品驱动增长（PLG）是一种增长模式：产品本身成为用户获取、激活、留存、扩张或变现的重要驱动力，通常与自助式体验相结合。

## Why It Matters

PLG changes how teams design acquisition and conversion by making product experience part of the growth engine.

## Example

A SaaS product lets users start with a free workspace, experience core value, and upgrade when they need additional collaboration or capacity.
