# Product-Market Fit (PMF)

## Definition

Product-market fit describes a condition in which a product satisfies a strong, sustained need for a defined market segment, reflected in behavior, retention, demand, and willingness to pay.

## 中文定义

产品市场契合是与“Product-Market Fit (PMF)”对应的专业概念，用于描述该术语所代表的业务、工程、数据、实验或增长机制。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Product-Market Fit (PMF) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Product-Market Fit (PMF) to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
