# Product-Qualified Account (PQA)

## Definition

A B2B account identified as sales-ready or expansion-ready based primarily on meaningful product-usage signals at the account level.

## 中文定义

产品合格账户是在 B2B 场景中，依据账户级产品使用信号判断已经具备购买、升级或销售介入价值的账户。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Product-Qualified Account (PQA) by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
