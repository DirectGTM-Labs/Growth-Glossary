# Product Roadmap

## Definition

A product roadmap communicates planned product outcomes, themes, priorities, or initiatives over time; it is not necessarily a fixed delivery schedule.

## 中文定义

产品路线大饼图。接下一年每季度画出来到底要造出点什么高科技安抚投资人的。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Product Roadmap helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Product Roadmap to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
