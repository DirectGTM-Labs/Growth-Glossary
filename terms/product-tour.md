# Product Tour

## Definition

A guided in-product sequence that introduces important features, workflows, or concepts to a user.

## 中文定义

产品导览是在产品内部通过一组引导步骤介绍关键功能、概念或工作流的新用户教育方式。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Product Tour to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
