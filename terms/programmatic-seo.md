# Programmatic SEO (pSEO)

## Definition

Programmatic SEO (pSEO) is the systematic generation of search-oriented pages from structured data and templates, used when many distinct pages provide genuine user value.

## 中文定义

程序化 SEO是与“Programmatic SEO (pSEO)”对应的专业概念，用于描述该术语所代表的业务、工程、数据、实验或增长机制。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Programmatic SEO (pSEO) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Programmatic SEO (pSEO) into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
