# Progressive Profiling

## Definition

A data-collection approach that gathers customer profile information gradually across interactions instead of requesting everything at once.

## 中文定义

渐进式用户画像收集是在多次交互中逐步获取用户资料，而不是一次表单要求用户填写所有信息。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Progressive Profiling to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
