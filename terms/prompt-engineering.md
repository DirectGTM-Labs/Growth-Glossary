# Prompt Engineering

## Definition

Prompt engineering is the systematic design and testing of model inputs, instructions, context, examples, and constraints to improve the reliability of LLM outputs.

## 中文定义

跟 AI 进行代码级沟通的技术。使用设定角色、规定输入输出格式、喂入少量示例（Few-Shot）让 AI 表现极度稳定。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Prompt Engineering helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Prompt Engineering into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
