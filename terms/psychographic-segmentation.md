# Psychographic Segmentation

## Definition

Psychographic segmentation groups audiences by attitudes, values, motivations, lifestyles, or related psychological characteristics.

## 中文定义

心理变量人群切片。根据“他是否天天处在房贷焦虑下”而不是死板的“他今年几岁”来分组。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Psychographic Segmentation helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Psychographic Segmentation to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
