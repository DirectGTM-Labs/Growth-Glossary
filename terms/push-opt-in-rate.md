# Push Opt-In Rate

## Definition

The percentage of eligible users who grant permission to receive push notifications.

## 中文定义

推送通知授权率是在被系统提出推送权限请求的合格用户中，最终允许接收推送通知的比例。

## Formula

Push Opt-In Rate = Users Granting Permission / Eligible Users Prompted

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Push Opt-In Rate in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
