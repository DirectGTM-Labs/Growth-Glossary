# Qualitative / Quantitative Balance (Qual/Quant Balance)

## Definition

Qualitative research explores experiences, motivations, perceptions, and reasons, while quantitative analysis measures patterns, frequencies, magnitudes, and relationships in numerical data. A balanced growth practice uses each for questions it can answer well.

## 中文定义

定量与定性研究的平衡是指结合定性研究对体验、动机、认知和原因的探索，与定量分析对规模、频率、幅度和关系的测量，让两类证据互相补充。

## Why It Matters

Quantitative data can identify where a problem occurs, while qualitative evidence can help explain why it occurs and generate better hypotheses.

## Example

Funnel analytics shows a large drop at onboarding step three; user interviews reveal that the required information creates a trust concern.
