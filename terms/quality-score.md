# Quality Score

## Definition

A diagnostic Google Ads score that summarizes how an ad, keyword, and landing page compare with other advertisers for expected click-through rate, ad relevance, and landing-page experience.

## 中文定义

质量得分是 Google Ads 的诊断性指标，用于比较关键词、广告和落地页在预期点击率、广告相关性和落地页体验方面相对于其他广告主的表现。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Quality Score alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
