# SaaS Quick Ratio

## Definition

A growth-health metric that compares additions to recurring revenue with losses from churn and contraction over the same period.

## 中文定义

SaaS 快速增长比率用于比较经常性收入的新增与扩张规模，相对于流失与收缩规模的大小，从而判断订阅业务的增长质量。

## Formula

SaaS Quick Ratio = (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Churned MRR + Contraction MRR)

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track SaaS Quick Ratio by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
