# Rage Click

## Definition

A pattern of rapid repeated clicks or taps on the same area that can indicate user frustration or an unresponsive interface.

## 中文定义

愤怒点击是用户在同一区域快速连续点击或触摸的行为模式，通常提示界面未响应或用户遭遇挫折。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Rage Click to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
