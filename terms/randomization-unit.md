# Randomization Unit

## Definition

The entity randomly assigned to experimental conditions, such as a user, account, session, device, region, or time block.

## 中文定义

随机化单位是被随机分配到不同实验条件的实体，可以是用户、账户、设备、地区、会话或时间块。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Randomization Unit in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
