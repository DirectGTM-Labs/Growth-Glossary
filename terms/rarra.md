# RARRA

## Definition

RARRA is a retention-first reframing of the traditional AARRR sequence, emphasizing retention before aggressive acquisition when product value is not yet durable.

## 中文定义

AARRR 的反转。强调绝对要以 Retention（留存）为第一优先级。水桶修补好之前，决不向里倒水（买流量）。

## Why It Matters

Understanding RARRA helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use RARRA to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
