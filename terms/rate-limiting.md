# Rate Limiting

## Definition

Rate limiting constrains how frequently a client, user, IP, token, or service may perform an operation within a defined interval.

## 中文定义

一分钟调取 API 超过 60 次直接踢飞封禁，防止羊毛党用爬虫刷爆你的服务器。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Rate Limiting helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Rate Limiting into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
