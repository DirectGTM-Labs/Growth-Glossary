# Reach

## Definition

The number of unique people or accounts exposed to a piece of content, campaign, or advertisement during a measurement period.

## 中文定义

触达人数是在规定时间内看到某项内容、广告或活动的去重用户或账户数量。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply Reach by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
