# User Reactivation

## Definition

The process of bringing an inactive or lapsed user back to meaningful product usage through product, lifecycle, or channel interventions.

## 中文定义

用户再激活是通过产品机制、生命周期触达或渠道运营，让已经沉默或流失的用户重新恢复有价值使用的过程。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track User Reactivation by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
