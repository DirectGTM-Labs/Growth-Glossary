# Reciprocity

## Definition

Reciprocity is the social tendency to respond to a perceived benefit or favor with a return action, which can influence relationship and conversion behavior.

## 中文定义

我先免费送你极其高质量的 3 万字白皮书，你白拿了心里有愧，顺手留个电话号码的转化率极高。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Reciprocity helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Reciprocity to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
