# Recommendation Engine

## Definition

A system that ranks and selects items, content, or actions for a user based on signals such as behavior, similarity, context, or predicted preference.

## 中文定义

推荐引擎是利用用户行为、相似度、上下文或预测模型，对内容、商品或下一步动作进行排序和选择的系统。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Recommendation Engine in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
