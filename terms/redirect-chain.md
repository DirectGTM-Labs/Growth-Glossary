# Redirect Chain

## Definition

A sequence in which one redirect points to another redirect before reaching the final destination.

## 中文定义

重定向链是一个 URL 重定向到另一个重定向 URL，连续经过多跳后才到达最终页面的情况。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Redirect Chain during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
