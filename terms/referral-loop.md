# Referral Loop

## Definition

A growth loop in which existing users create invitations, recommendations, or shareable outputs that acquire additional users.

## 中文定义

推荐循环是现有用户通过邀请、分享、推荐或可传播产出带来新用户，并由新用户继续产生下一轮推荐的增长循环。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Referral Loop by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
