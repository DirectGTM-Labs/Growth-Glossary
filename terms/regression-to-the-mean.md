# Regression to the Mean

## Definition

The tendency for unusually extreme observations to be followed by observations closer to the average when part of the extremity was caused by random variation.

## 中文定义

均值回归是极端观察值中包含随机波动时，后续观测往往更接近总体平均水平的统计现象。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Regression to the Mean in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
