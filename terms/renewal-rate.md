# Renewal Rate

## Definition

The percentage of eligible contracts, subscriptions, customers, or revenue that renew during a defined period.

## 中文定义

续约率是到期可续约的客户、合同、订阅或收入中，最终完成续约的比例，具体分母必须明确。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Renewal Rate in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
