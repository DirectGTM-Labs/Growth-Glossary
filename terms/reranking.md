# Reranking

## Definition

A retrieval step that reorders an initial candidate set using a more precise relevance model before final context or results are selected.

## 中文定义

重排序是在初始检索得到候选结果后，再用更精细的相关性模型重新排列候选结果顺序的检索阶段。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate Reranking across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
