# Responsive Search Ad (RSA)

## Definition

A Search ad format in which multiple headlines and descriptions are provided and Google combines assets dynamically for eligible auctions.

## 中文定义

自适应搜索广告是向 Google 提供多个标题和描述素材，由系统在不同竞价中动态组合展示的搜索广告格式。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Responsive Search Ad (RSA) alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
