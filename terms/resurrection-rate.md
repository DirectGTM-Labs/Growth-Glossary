# Resurrection Rate

## Definition

The percentage of previously inactive or churned users who return and perform a defined meaningful activity within a measurement period.

## 中文定义

复活率是指曾经不活跃或已经流失的用户中，在统计周期内重新回来并完成规定有效行为的比例。

## Formula

Resurrection Rate = Resurrected Users / Eligible Inactive or Churned Users

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Resurrection Rate by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
