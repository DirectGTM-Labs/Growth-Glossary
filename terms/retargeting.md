# Retargeting

## Definition

Retargeting delivers advertising or other marketing messages to audiences based on prior interactions with a brand, product, site, or campaign.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Retargeting helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

For example, a growth team could use Retargeting to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
