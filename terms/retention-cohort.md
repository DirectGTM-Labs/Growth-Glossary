# Retention Cohort

## Definition

A retention cohort groups users who share a defined starting event or time period and tracks their subsequent retention behavior.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Retention Cohort helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Retention Cohort to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
