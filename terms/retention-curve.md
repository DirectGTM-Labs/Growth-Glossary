# Retention Curve

## Definition

A retention curve shows the proportion of a defined cohort that remains active or retained as a function of time since acquisition or activation.

## 中文定义

随时间推移留下的用户百分比曲线。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Retention Curve helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Retention Curve to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
