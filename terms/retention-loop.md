# Retention Loop

## Definition

A self-reinforcing mechanism in which recurring product value or user-created assets increase the likelihood of future return.

## 中文定义

留存循环是产品持续提供价值或沉淀用户资产，使用户未来再次返回的概率不断提高的反馈机制。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Retention Loop by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
