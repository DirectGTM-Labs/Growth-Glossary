# Retention Rate

## Definition

The proportion of a defined cohort that remains active or returns to perform a specified meaningful action after a given period.

## 中文定义

留存率是某一初始用户群体中，在规定时间后仍保持活跃或再次完成指定有效行为的用户比例。

## Formula

Retention Rate = Retained Users / Users in Starting Cohort

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Retention Rate by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
