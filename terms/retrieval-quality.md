# Retrieval Quality

## Definition

The degree to which a retrieval system returns relevant, sufficient, diverse, and trustworthy evidence for a query or downstream generation task.

## 中文定义

检索质量表示一个检索系统返回结果在相关性、覆盖度、证据充分性、多样性和可信度等方面的综合水平。

## Why It Matters

It affects how AI and retrieval systems find, select, ground, rank, or cite information, which is increasingly important for AI-mediated discovery.

## Example

A GEO or AI-search team could evaluate Retrieval Quality across a fixed query set and compare retrieval, citation, or answer-quality changes after content or data improvements.
