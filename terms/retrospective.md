# Retrospective

## Definition

A retrospective is a structured review conducted after a project, experiment, or work cycle to examine outcomes, what was learned, what went well, what did not, and what process changes should follow.

## 中文定义

回顾复盘（Retrospective）是在项目、实验或工作周期结束后，通过结构化方式总结结果、学习、做得好的地方、问题以及下一步流程改进的活动。

## Why It Matters

Retrospectives convert individual outcomes into reusable organizational learning and process improvements.

## Example

After an experiment, the team records the result, unexpected data-quality issues, decision quality, and one or two concrete process changes.
