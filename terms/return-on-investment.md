# Return on Investment (ROI)

## Definition

Return on investment (ROI) compares the net return from an investment with the investment cost; unlike ROAS, it is not limited to advertising spend.

## 中文定义

投资回报率是与“Return on Investment (ROI)”对应的专业概念，用于描述该术语所代表的业务、工程、数据、实验或增长机制。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Return on Investment (ROI) helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Return on Investment (ROI) to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
