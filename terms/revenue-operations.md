# Revenue Operations (RevOps)

## Definition

A cross-functional operating model that aligns revenue-related processes, data, technology, and teams across marketing, sales, customer success, and finance.

## 中文定义

收入运营是一种跨职能运营框架，把营销、销售、客户成功与财务中的收入流程、数据、技术和团队机制统一起来，以提升整个收入生命周期的效率和可预测性。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Revenue Operations (RevOps) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
