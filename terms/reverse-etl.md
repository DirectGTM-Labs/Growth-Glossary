# Reverse ETL

## Definition

Reverse ETL moves modeled or enriched data from an analytical warehouse back into operational tools such as CRM, marketing automation, or customer-success systems.

## 中文定义

把数仓里高级分析得出的结果（如“用户流失概率90%”），推回到一线的营销 SaaS（如 HubSpot）里，触发自动发挽留邮件。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Reverse ETL helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Reverse ETL into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
