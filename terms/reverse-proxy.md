# Reverse Proxy

## Definition

A reverse proxy receives client requests on behalf of backend services and can route, cache, secure, transform, or load-balance those requests.

## 中文定义

挡在真实服务器前面的替身（如 Nginx），负责挡黑客攻击和把流量分发给后面没那么累的机器。
