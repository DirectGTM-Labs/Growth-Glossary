# Reverse Trial

## Definition

A reverse trial starts a user with paid or full-feature access and later transitions them to a restricted/free tier if they do not convert.

## 中文定义

进门是 Freemium 免费版，但第一周硬塞给你极其昂贵的企业版功能体验，试用期过后再把这些特权活生生剥夺掉。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Reverse Trial helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Reverse Trial to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
