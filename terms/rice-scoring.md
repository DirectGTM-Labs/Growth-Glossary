# RICE Scoring

## Definition

RICE scoring extends ICE by adding Reach, typically using a formula that combines Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort.

## 中文定义

ICE 进阶版。增加了一个“触达面（Reach）”，再牛逼的点子如果一年只有 10 个人能看到，总分也会被无情打低。

## Why It Matters

Understanding RICE Scoring helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use RICE Scoring to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
