# Rich Results

## Definition

Search results enhanced by structured data or other supported information to display additional visual or interactive features.

## 中文定义

富媒体搜索结果是利用结构化数据等信息展示额外视觉、交互或语义元素的增强型搜索结果。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Rich Results during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
