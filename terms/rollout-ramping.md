# Rollout / Ramping

## Definition

Rollout or ramping is the controlled process of increasing the proportion of users or traffic exposed to a software change, often with monitoring and rollback criteria.

## 中文定义

灰度发布/流量扩量（Rollout / Ramping）是逐步增加用户或流量暴露于软件变化的比例，并配合监控和回滚标准的发布过程。

## Why It Matters

Progressive exposure limits blast radius and provides opportunities to detect technical or business problems before full release.

## Example

A feature is released to 1%, then 5%, 25%, and 100% of eligible users after predefined health checks pass.
