# Round-Robin Routing

## Definition

A lead- or account-assignment method that distributes records sequentially among eligible owners to balance workload.

## 中文定义

轮询分配是按照预定顺序把新线索或账户轮流分配给符合条件的负责人，以尽量平衡工作量。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Round-Robin Routing in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
