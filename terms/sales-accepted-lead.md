# Sales Accepted Lead (SAL)

## Definition

A marketing- or system-qualified lead that the sales team has formally accepted for active follow-up.

## 中文定义

销售接受线索是由营销或系统识别后，已经被销售团队正式接收并进入实际跟进流程的线索。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Sales Accepted Lead (SAL) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
