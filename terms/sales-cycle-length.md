# Sales Cycle Length

## Definition

The average elapsed time required for an opportunity to move from a defined starting stage to closed-won or closed-lost.

## 中文定义

销售周期是商机从规定起点进入销售流程，到最终赢单或输单所经历的平均时间。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Sales Cycle Length in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
