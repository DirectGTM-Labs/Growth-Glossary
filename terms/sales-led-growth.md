# Sales-Led Growth

## Definition

Sales-led growth is a model in which a sales organization is a primary mechanism for acquiring, converting, and expanding customers, often through high-touch processes.

## 中文定义

依靠销售团队打单驱动的传统增长模式。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Sales-Led Growth helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Sales-Led Growth to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
