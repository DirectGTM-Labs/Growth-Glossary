# Sales Pipeline

## Definition

The set of active revenue opportunities organized by defined sales stages from qualification through close.

## 中文定义

销售管道是按照明确销售阶段组织的所有活跃销售商机集合，用于管理未来可能产生的收入。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Sales Pipeline in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
