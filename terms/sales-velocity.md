# Sales Velocity

## Definition

A measure of how quickly qualified pipeline is converted into revenue, commonly using opportunity count, average deal value, win rate, and sales-cycle length.

## 中文定义

销售速度衡量销售管道把商机转化为收入的速度，常综合商机数量、平均交易金额、赢单率和销售周期。

## Formula

Sales Velocity = (Number of Opportunities × Average Deal Value × Win Rate) / Average Sales Cycle Length

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Sales Velocity in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
