# Scarcity Principle

## Definition

The scarcity principle describes the tendency for perceived limited availability to increase the perceived value or urgency of an option in some contexts.

## 中文定义

稀缺原则。“限量 10 份，永远绝版”，哪怕它是一坨无用的贴纸，估价都会因此无限上升。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Scarcity Principle helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Scarcity Principle to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
