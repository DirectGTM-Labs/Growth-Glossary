# Experiment Scorecard

## Definition

An experiment scorecard is a structured specification and evaluation record that defines the primary outcome metric, secondary metrics, guardrail metrics, and often the experiment hypothesis and decision criteria.

## 中文定义

实验记分卡是实验的结构化说明和评估记录，用于定义主要结果指标、次要指标、护栏指标，以及通常包括假设和决策标准。

## Why It Matters

It prevents teams from judging experiments solely by one favorable metric and makes evaluation criteria explicit before results are known.

## Example

A checkout experiment uses completed purchases as the primary metric, average order value as secondary, and payment-error rate as a guardrail.
