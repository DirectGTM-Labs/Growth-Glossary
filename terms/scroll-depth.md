# Scroll Depth

## Definition

A measure of how far users scroll through a page, commonly expressed as pixels, sections, or percentage of content.

## 中文定义

滚动深度衡量用户在页面中实际向下浏览到多远，可以按像素、区块或内容百分比计算。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Scroll Depth to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
