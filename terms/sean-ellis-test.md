# Sean Ellis Test

## Definition

The Sean Ellis test is a product-value survey popularized by Sean Ellis that asks users how disappointed they would be if they could no longer use a product; it is a heuristic, not a universal PMF threshold.

## 中文定义

测量产品市场契合度的问卷工具。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Sean Ellis Test helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Sean Ellis Test to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
