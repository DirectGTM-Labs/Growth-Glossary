# Search Impression Share

## Definition

The percentage of eligible Search impressions an advertiser actually received.

## 中文定义

搜索展示份额是实际获得的搜索广告展示次数占估算有资格获得展示次数的比例。

## Formula

Search Impression Share = Impressions Received / Estimated Eligible Impressions

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Search Impression Share alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
