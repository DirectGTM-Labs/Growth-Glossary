# Search Terms Report

## Definition

A Google Ads report showing the user search terms that triggered ads, subject to platform reporting and privacy thresholds.

## 中文定义

搜索词报告展示真实用户查询中哪些搜索词触发了广告，具体可见内容受平台隐私与报告阈值影响。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Search Terms Report alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
