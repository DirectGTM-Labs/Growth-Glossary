# Seasonality Adjustment

## Definition

A Smart Bidding control used for short, predictable events when conversion rates are expected to deviate materially from normal patterns.

## 中文定义

季节性调整是智能出价中的控制机制，用于告知系统短期且可预测的事件会使转化率显著偏离正常水平。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Seasonality Adjustment alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
