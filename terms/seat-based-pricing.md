# Seat-Based Pricing

## Definition

A pricing model in which charges scale primarily with the number of licensed or active users, seats, or members.

## 中文定义

按席位定价是主要根据获得授权、活跃或购买许可的用户、成员或席位数量计费的模式。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Seat-Based Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
