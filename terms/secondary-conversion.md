# Secondary Conversion

## Definition

A conversion action kept for observation or supplemental reporting rather than primary bidding optimization.

## 中文定义

次要转化是主要用于观察和辅助报告，而不作为核心自动出价优化目标的转化操作。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review Secondary Conversion alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
