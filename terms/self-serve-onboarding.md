# Self-serve Onboarding

## Definition

Self-serve onboarding enables users to learn, configure, and reach initial value without requiring a human representative to guide each step.

## 中文定义

PLG 模式的核心，系统像个贴身导购一样，让一个小白能在完全没人教的情况下 3 分钟跑通核心功能。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Self-serve Onboarding helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Self-serve Onboarding to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
