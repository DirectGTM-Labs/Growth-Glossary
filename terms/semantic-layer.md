# Semantic Layer

## Definition

A semantic layer provides governed business definitions, metrics, dimensions, and relationships between analytical concepts and underlying data models.

## 中文定义

在乱七八糟的物理数据库上面盖一层字典，告诉 AI “Revenue”的计算公式是什么，防止 AI 瞎算账。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Semantic Layer helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

For example, a growth team could incorporate Semantic Layer into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
