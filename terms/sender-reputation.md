# Sender Reputation

## Definition

The reputation signals associated with a sending domain, IP, or stream that mailbox providers use when making delivery decisions.

## 中文定义

发件人信誉是邮箱服务商根据发送域名、IP 和历史发送行为形成的一组信誉信号，用于辅助投递判断。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Sender Reputation in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
