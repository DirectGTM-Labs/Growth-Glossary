# Sentiment Analysis

## Definition

Sentiment analysis classifies or estimates the affective polarity or emotional tone expressed in text or other user-generated content.

## 中文定义

把网上的几十万条评论塞给 AI 判卷，不用人看，系统自动判断大盘里有多少骂娘的、多少唱好的。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Sentiment Analysis helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Sentiment Analysis to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
