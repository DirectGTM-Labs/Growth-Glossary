# Sequential Testing

## Definition

Sequential testing is an experimental design and inference approach that explicitly accounts for repeated looks at accumulating data rather than treating them as independent final analyses.

## 中文定义

允许产品经理每天偷看实验结果，而且保证不会产生假阴阳性的高级统计算法。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Sequential Testing helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

For example, a growth team could incorporate Sequential Testing into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
