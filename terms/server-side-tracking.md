# Server-Side Tracking

## Definition

Server-side tracking collects or forwards measurement events from backend systems rather than relying solely on browser-side scripts.

## 中文定义

数据不在前端浏览器采集，而是在后端产生，确保 100% 准确性且无惧任何前端隐私拦截。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Server-Side Tracking helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
