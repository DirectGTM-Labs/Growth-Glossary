# Shadow Mode

## Definition

Shadow mode runs a new model, rule, or service alongside the production system without using its output to affect user-visible decisions.

## 中文定义

新系统悄悄在后台空转跑真实流量，但结果不对外输出，只用来和老系统对比验证是否会引发灾难。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Shadow Mode helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could incorporate Shadow Mode into an experiment, data pipeline, product system, or measurement workflow when the stated assumptions and implementation requirements are satisfied.
