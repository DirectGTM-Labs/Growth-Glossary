# Share of Voice

## Definition

Share of Voice (SOV) is a measure of a brand’s or entity’s proportion of relevant exposure, mentions, advertising presence, or conversation within a defined market or channel.

## 中文定义

在整个大行业（如跑鞋赛道）所有被提起的文章或社交媒体声量中，关于你的讨论占了百分之几的江山。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Share of Voice helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Share of Voice to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
