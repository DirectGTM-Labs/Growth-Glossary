# Social Listening

## Definition

Social listening systematically monitors public or permitted social conversations to identify mentions, themes, sentiment, needs, and emerging issues.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Social Listening helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Social Listening to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
