# Social Selling

## Definition

The use of social networks to identify, research, engage, and build relationships with prospective buyers as part of the sales process.

## 中文定义

社交销售是销售人员利用社交网络寻找、研究、互动并建立潜在买家关系，以推动后续销售机会的做法。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply Social Selling by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
