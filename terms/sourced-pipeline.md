# Sourced Pipeline

## Definition

Pipeline whose creation is attributed to a specific team, channel, or program under an agreed sourcing rule.

## 中文定义

来源管道是按照双方约定的归因规则，认定由某一团队、渠道或营销项目直接创造出来的销售管道。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Sourced Pipeline in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
