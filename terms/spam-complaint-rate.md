# Spam Complaint Rate

## Definition

The percentage of delivered messages that recipients mark as spam or junk.

## 中文定义

垃圾邮件投诉率是已经成功投递的邮件中，被收件人主动标记为垃圾邮件或举报的比例。

## Formula

Spam Complaint Rate = Spam Complaints / Delivered Messages

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Spam Complaint Rate in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
