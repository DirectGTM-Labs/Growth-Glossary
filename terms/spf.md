# Sender Policy Framework (SPF)

## Definition

An email-authentication mechanism that lets a domain publish which mail servers are authorized to send email on its behalf.

## 中文定义

SPF 是邮件认证机制，允许域名通过 DNS 声明哪些邮件服务器被授权代表该域名发送邮件。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Sender Policy Framework (SPF) in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
