# Spillover Effect

## Definition

An effect of a treatment on units other than the unit directly assigned to that treatment.

## 中文定义

溢出效应是某个实验单位接受处理后，对其他没有直接接受该处理的单位结果产生影响。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Spillover Effect in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
