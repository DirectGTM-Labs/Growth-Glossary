# Standard Deviation

## Definition

Standard deviation measures the dispersion of observations around their mean.

## 中文定义

衡量你实验数据波动的剧烈程度，波动越大，说明效果越不稳定。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Standard Deviation helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
