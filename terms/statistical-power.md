# Statistical Power

## Definition

Statistical power is the probability that a statistical test will reject a specified null hypothesis when a particular alternative effect is truly present, under the assumed model and design.

## 中文定义

统计功效是当某个特定的真实效应存在时，统计检验在给定模型和设计下成功拒绝指定原假设的概率。

## Why It Matters

Adequate power reduces the risk of failing to detect effects that matter under the experiment's assumptions.

## Example

An experiment is designed with 80% power to detect its pre-specified minimum detectable effect.
