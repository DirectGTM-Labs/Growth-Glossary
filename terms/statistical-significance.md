# Statistical Significance

## Definition

Statistical significance is a decision concept describing whether observed data provide sufficient evidence against a specified null hypothesis under a chosen statistical test and significance level.

## 中文定义

统计显著性是统计推断中的一个决策概念，用于描述在给定检验和显著性水平下，观测数据是否对某个原假设提供了足够反对证据。

## Why It Matters

It helps quantify evidence against a null hypothesis, but statistical significance alone does not establish practical importance, causal validity, or business value.

## Example

An experiment result is statistically significant at a pre-specified alpha level, but the estimated effect is so small that the team decides it is not practically meaningful.
