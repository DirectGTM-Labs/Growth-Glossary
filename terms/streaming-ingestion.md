# Streaming Ingestion

## Definition

Streaming ingestion continuously captures and transports data events with low latency so that downstream systems can process them soon after they are produced.

## 中文定义

流式摄取是持续捕获并低延迟传输数据事件，使下游系统能够在数据产生后较快处理它们。

## Why It Matters

It supports use cases where fresh data matters, such as real-time monitoring, operational decisions, and event-driven systems.

## Example

Application events are published to Kafka and consumed by a stream-processing service within seconds.
