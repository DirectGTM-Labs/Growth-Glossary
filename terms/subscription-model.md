# Subscription Business Model

## Definition

A recurring-revenue model in which customers pay periodically for continued access to a product or service.

## 中文定义

订阅商业模式是客户按照固定周期持续付费，以保持对产品或服务访问权的经常性收入模式。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Subscription Business Model when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
