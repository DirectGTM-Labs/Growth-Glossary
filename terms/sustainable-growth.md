# Sustainable Growth

## Definition

Sustainable growth is growth that can continue over time without relying on structurally destructive economics, excessive dependency on temporary tactics, or deteriorating customer value.

## 中文定义

不靠烧投资人的钱补贴买数据，而是跑通商业闭环，赚一毛钱花八分的健康复利状态。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Sustainable Growth helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Sustainable Growth to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
