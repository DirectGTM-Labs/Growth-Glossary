# Stable Unit Treatment Value Assumption (SUTVA)

## Definition

A causal-inference assumption that each unit's potential outcome depends only on its own assigned treatment and that each treatment condition is well defined.

## 中文定义

稳定单元处理值假设是因果推断中的重要假设，要求一个单位的潜在结果只取决于其自身处理，并且处理条件本身定义清晰。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Stable Unit Treatment Value Assumption (SUTVA) in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
