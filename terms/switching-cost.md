# Switching Cost

## Definition

Switching costs are financial, operational, learning, contractual, data, or psychological costs associated with moving from one provider or product to another.

## 中文定义

转换成本。数据、回忆、熟练度全陷在这个软件里，让他跑去用更好用的竞品简直是要他割肉般的剧痛。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Switching Cost helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Switching Cost to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
