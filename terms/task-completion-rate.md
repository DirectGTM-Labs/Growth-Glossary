# Task Completion Rate

## Definition

The percentage of observed or tested users who successfully complete a specified task.

## 中文定义

任务完成率是在被观察或测试的用户中，成功完成指定任务的用户比例。

## Formula

Task Completion Rate = Users Completing Task / Users Attempting Task

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use Task Completion Rate to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
