# Technical SEO

## Definition

Technical SEO improves crawlability, indexability, rendering, site architecture, performance, and other technical aspects that affect search accessibility and discoverability.

## 中文定义

技术 SEO。给机器抓取的纯代码优化，包括重写 sitemap、干掉白屏渲染问题，属于硬核基建。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Technical SEO helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Technical SEO to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
