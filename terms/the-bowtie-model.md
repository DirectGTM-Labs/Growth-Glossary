# The Bowtie Model

## Definition

The bowtie model extends the acquisition-to-conversion funnel by emphasizing post-purchase retention, expansion, advocacy, and other lifecycle outcomes.

## Why It Matters

Understanding The Bowtie Model helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use The Bowtie Model to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
