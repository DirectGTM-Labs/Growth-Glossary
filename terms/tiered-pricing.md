# Tiered Pricing

## Definition

A pricing structure that offers predefined plans or price bands with different features, limits, or rates.

## 中文定义

分层定价是预先设计多个不同价格、功能或使用额度的套餐层级供客户选择。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Tiered Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
