# Topic Cluster

## Definition

A content architecture that organizes related pages around a central topic or hub and links them together to improve navigation and topical coverage.

## 中文定义

主题集群是围绕一个核心主题组织多篇相关内容，并通过内部链接形成中心页面与支持页面关系的内容架构。

## Why It Matters

It affects how search systems discover, render, understand, index, or present content and therefore influences organic discoverability and technical search health.

## Example

An SEO team could audit Topic Cluster during a technical review and verify the result with crawler diagnostics, server logs, or Search Console where applicable.
