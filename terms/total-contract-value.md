# Total Contract Value (TCV)

## Definition

The total committed value of a contract over its full term, including recurring and, depending on the business definition, certain one-time fees.

## 中文定义

合同总价值是合同整个有效期内承诺的总价值，具体口径可以包含经常性费用以及业务定义允许计入的一次性费用。

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Total Contract Value (TCV) in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
