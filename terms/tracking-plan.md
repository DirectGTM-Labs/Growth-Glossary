# Tracking Plan

## Definition

A specification that defines which events and properties should be collected, why they matter, where they fire, and how they are named.

## 中文定义

埋点规划是一份明确“追踪哪些事件和属性、为什么追踪、在哪里触发、如何命名”的数据采集规范。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Tracking Plan in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
