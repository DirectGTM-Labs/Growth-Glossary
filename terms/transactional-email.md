# Transactional Email

## Definition

A message primarily triggered by a user's transaction or account activity, such as receipts, password resets, or shipping notifications.

## 中文定义

事务邮件是由用户交易或账户行为直接触发的服务型邮件，例如收据、密码重置和物流通知。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Transactional Email in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
