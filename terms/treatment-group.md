# Treatment Group

## Definition

The experimental group exposed to the intervention whose outcomes are compared with the control condition.

## 中文定义

实验组是在受控实验中接受新干预的群体，其结果与对照条件进行比较。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Treatment Group in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
