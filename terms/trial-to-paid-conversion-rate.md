# Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate

## Definition

The percentage of trial users or accounts that begin a paid subscription before or after the trial ends.

## 中文定义

试用转付费率是进入试用的用户或账户中，在试用期间或结束后转为付费订阅的比例。

## Formula

Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate = Trial Users Converted to Paid / Eligible Trial Users

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
