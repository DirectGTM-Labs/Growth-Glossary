# Triggered Messaging

## Definition

Messages automatically sent when a defined event, state, threshold, or schedule condition is met.

## 中文定义

触发式消息是在某个事件、状态、阈值或时间条件满足时由系统自动发送的消息。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Triggered Messaging in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
