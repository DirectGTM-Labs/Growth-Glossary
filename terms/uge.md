# UGE

## Definition

User-generated experience (UGE) refers to product experiences, configurations, content, or interactive artifacts created by users themselves and potentially reused or shared.

## 中文定义

不光让用户发文字，更是让用户自己在你的平台里利用工具搭建 3D 虚拟展厅等可交互的沉浸场景资产。

## Why It Matters

Understanding UGE helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use UGE to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
