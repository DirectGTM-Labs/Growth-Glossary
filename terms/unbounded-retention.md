# Unbounded Retention

## Definition

A retention definition that counts a user as retained if they return at any time after a specified interval.

## 中文定义

无界留存是一种留存口径：只要用户在某个指定时间点之后的任意时间再次回来，就被视为该时间点上的留存用户。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Unbounded Retention by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
