# Uncanny Valley

## Definition

The uncanny valley is a drop in perceived affinity or comfort when an artificial representation becomes highly human-like but still contains salient cues of artificiality.

## 中文定义

恐怖谷效应。你用 AI 写得极其像真人但又在小细节上怪异无情时，这会让买家感到毛骨悚然而极其恶心地关掉一切逃离。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Uncanny Valley helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Uncanny Valley to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
