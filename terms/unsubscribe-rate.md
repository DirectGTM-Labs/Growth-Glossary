# Unsubscribe Rate

## Definition

The percentage of delivered or successfully sent marketing messages that result in recipients unsubscribing, according to the reporting definition.

## 中文定义

退订率是成功发送或投递的营销消息中，最终触发用户取消订阅的比例，具体分母需要在报表口径中明确。

## Formula

Unsubscribe Rate = Unsubscribes / Delivered Messages

## Why It Matters

It helps teams coordinate customer data and communications across the lifecycle while improving relevance, retention, and operational consistency.

## Example

A lifecycle team could operationalize Unsubscribe Rate in its customer-engagement stack so user behavior changes which message, channel, or experience is delivered next.
