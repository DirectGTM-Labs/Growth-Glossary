# Upsell

## Definition

Upsell is the sale of a higher-priced, higher-capacity, or more feature-rich version of an existing purchase or subscription.

## 中文定义

向上销售。去买基础款，店员极力吹嘘让你加钱换个大号旗舰版的套路。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Upsell helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Upsell to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
