# Usage-Based Pricing

## Definition

A pricing model in which charges scale with measured consumption of a product resource or service.

## 中文定义

按用量定价是费用随着客户实际消耗的产品资源、调用量、数据量或服务使用量变化的收费模式。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Usage-Based Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
