# Usage Trigger

## Definition

A product behavior or threshold that initiates an automated message, workflow, sales action, or in-product experience.

## 中文定义

使用触发信号是达到某个行为、次数、额度或状态后，自动启动消息、工作流、销售动作或产品体验的条件。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track Usage Trigger by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
