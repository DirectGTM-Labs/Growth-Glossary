# User Flow

## Definition

A sequence of steps or states a user moves through to complete a task or goal within a product or website.

## 中文定义

用户流程是用户为了完成某个任务或目标，在产品或网站中经过的一系列页面、状态和操作步骤。

## Why It Matters

It helps diagnose or reduce friction in user journeys and provides a concrete surface for improving conversion, activation, or task completion.

## Example

A CRO team could use User Flow to identify a specific source of friction, formulate a hypothesis, and validate the proposed change with behavioral evidence or an experiment.
