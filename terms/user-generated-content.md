# User-Generated Content (UGC)

## Definition

Content created by customers, users, fans, or community members rather than produced directly by the brand.

## 中文定义

用户生成内容是由客户、产品用户、粉丝或社区成员创作，而不是由品牌官方直接生产的内容。

## Why It Matters

It helps teams plan, distribute, or measure social and content growth while distinguishing reach from genuine engagement and business outcomes.

## Example

A growth marketing team could track or apply User-Generated Content (UGC) by channel and segment, then compare it with downstream traffic, conversion, pipeline, or retention rather than evaluating it in isolation.
