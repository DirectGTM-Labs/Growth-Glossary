# User Persona

## Definition

A user persona is a research-informed archetype representing a meaningful pattern of users, including goals, behaviors, needs, and constraints.

## 中文定义

营销部门画的一个虚拟稻草人。例如“小红，25岁，白领，焦虑，月光族”，所有文案全都对着小红这个靶子写。

## Why It Matters

Understanding User Persona helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use User Persona to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
