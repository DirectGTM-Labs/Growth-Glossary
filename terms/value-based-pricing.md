# Value-Based Pricing

## Definition

A pricing approach that sets price primarily according to customers' perceived or realized value rather than production cost alone.

## 中文定义

价值定价是主要根据客户感知或实际获得的价值，而不是单纯依据成本来确定价格的方法。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Value-Based Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
