# Value Proposition

## Definition

A value proposition states the specific value a product promises to deliver to a defined customer and why the offer is preferable to alternatives.

## 中文定义

价值主张。一句一击必杀的人话标语，告诉对方为什么买我而不买别人。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Value Proposition helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Value Proposition to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
