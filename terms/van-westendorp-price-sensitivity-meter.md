# Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter

## Definition

A survey-based pricing research method that uses four price-perception questions to estimate acceptable and psychologically relevant price ranges.

## 中文定义

Van Westendorp 价格敏感度计量是一种基于四个价格感知问题的调查方法，用来估算消费者认为可接受或具有心理临界意义的价格区间。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
