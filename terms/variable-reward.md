# Variable Reward

## Definition

Variable reward is a reinforcement pattern in which the outcome of an action varies, potentially increasing repeated engagement by introducing uncertainty.

## 中文定义

让用户产生多巴胺、对产品上瘾的未知奖励。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Variable Reward helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Variable Reward to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
