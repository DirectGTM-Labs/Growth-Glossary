# Experiment Variant

## Definition

A specific version of an experience, algorithm, message, or treatment included in an experiment.

## 中文定义

实验变体是同一实验中被比较的某个具体版本，例如不同页面、算法、消息或处理方案。

## Why It Matters

It is part of designing, operating, or interpreting controlled experiments with enough rigor to distinguish causal signal from noise.

## Example

An experimentation team could document Experiment Variant in the experiment design or analysis plan before launch so the resulting decision is statistically and operationally defensible.
