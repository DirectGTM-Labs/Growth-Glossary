# Viral Coefficient

## Definition

The viral coefficient estimates how many additional users are generated per existing user through referral mechanisms, often incorporating invitation volume and conversion.

## 中文定义

结合了 K-Factor 和拉新周期时长的高级算法，不仅看拉几个人，更看是花一天拉来的，还是花一年拉来的。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Viral Coefficient helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Viral Coefficient to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
