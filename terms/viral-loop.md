# Viral Loop

## Definition

A viral loop is a product or distribution mechanism in which existing users generate invitations, content, or other outputs that expose the product to prospective new users, some of whom become users and repeat the process.

## 中文定义

病毒循环是一种产品或分发机制：现有用户产生邀请、内容或其他可传播输出，使潜在新用户接触产品，其中一部分转化后再次产生传播，从而形成循环。

## Formula

A simplified viral coefficient is K = Invitations per User × Invitation-to-User Conversion Rate.

## Why It Matters

A functioning viral loop can lower dependence on paid acquisition when user-generated distribution creates incremental acquisition.

## Example

A collaboration tool creates shareable project invitations; invited recipients who activate the product can later invite their own collaborators.
