# Volume Pricing

## Definition

A pricing approach in which unit price changes as purchased quantity or usage volume crosses defined thresholds.

## 中文定义

阶梯量价定价是随着采购数量或使用量达到不同区间，单位价格按预先设置的阶梯发生变化的方式。

## Why It Matters

It shapes how a business captures value, aligns price with customer willingness to pay, and manages monetization and unit economics.

## Example

A monetization team could use Volume Pricing when testing how different packages or price structures affect conversion, expansion, revenue, and customer value.
