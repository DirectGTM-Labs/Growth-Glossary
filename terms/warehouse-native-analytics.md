# Warehouse-Native Analytics

## Definition

An analytics architecture that queries or models data directly in the cloud data warehouse rather than requiring a separate proprietary copy as the primary analytical store.

## 中文定义

数仓原生分析是直接基于云数据仓库中的数据执行查询、建模和分析，而不是把数据首先复制到另一个专有分析存储中的架构方式。

## Why It Matters

It improves the reliability, governance, and operational usability of data that growth, marketing, product, and revenue systems depend on.

## Example

A growth engineering team could define and monitor Warehouse-Native Analytics so analytics, activation, and experimentation use consistent and trustworthy data.
