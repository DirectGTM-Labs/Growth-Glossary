# WAU/MAU Ratio

## Definition

The ratio of weekly active users to monthly active users, used as a frequency-of-use indicator for products with roughly weekly usage patterns.

## 中文定义

WAU/MAU 比率是周活跃用户数除以月活跃用户数，用于衡量以周为典型使用周期的产品，其用户使用频率和粘性。

## Formula

WAU/MAU Ratio = Weekly Active Users / Monthly Active Users

## Why It Matters

It helps teams connect product behavior to activation, engagement, retention, or monetization so growth decisions are based on measurable user value rather than surface activity.

## Example

A product growth team could track WAU/MAU Ratio by cohort and compare it with downstream retention or monetization before deciding whether an onboarding or product change improved durable user value.
