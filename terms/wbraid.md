# WBRAID

## Definition

A privacy-preserving Google Ads identifier used in supported web-to-app or web measurement scenarios under privacy constraints.

## 中文定义

WBRAID 是 Google Ads 在部分网页与应用转化链路的隐私受限场景中使用的隐私保护型标识符。

## Why It Matters

It directly affects paid-search eligibility, delivery, optimization, measurement, or efficiency and therefore influences acquisition economics.

## Example

A paid acquisition team could review WBRAID alongside conversion quality, CPA, and ROAS before changing bids, budgets, targeting, or measurement settings.
