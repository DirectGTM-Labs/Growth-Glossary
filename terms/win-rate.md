# Win Rate

## Definition

The percentage of qualified sales opportunities that end in a closed-won outcome.

## 中文定义

赢单率是已经形成明确销售结果的商机中，最终成为 Closed-Won 的比例。

## Formula

Win Rate = Closed-Won Opportunities / Closed Opportunities

## Why It Matters

It helps align go-to-market execution with pipeline quality, revenue efficiency, forecasting, and cross-functional accountability.

## Example

A B2B growth team could include Win Rate in its weekly revenue review to identify whether pipeline creation, conversion, retention, or forecast quality is improving.
