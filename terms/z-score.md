# Z-score

## Definition

A z-score expresses an observation’s distance from a reference mean in units of standard deviation.

## 中文定义

一个数据离全大盘平均线偏离了多少个标准差的绝对距离标尺。

## Why It Matters

Understanding Z-score helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.
