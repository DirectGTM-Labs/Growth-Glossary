# Zero-click Searches

## Definition

Zero-click searches are search interactions in which the user obtains a satisfactory answer or action from the results interface without clicking through to an external result.

## Why It Matters

Understanding Zero-click Searches helps a growth practitioner make clearer decisions, define measurement correctly, and avoid confusing a useful tactic or implementation detail with a universal growth law.

## Example

For example, a growth team could use Zero-click Searches to diagnose, design, measure, or improve a defined stage of the customer or product lifecycle.
